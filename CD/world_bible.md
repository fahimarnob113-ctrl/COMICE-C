## 🌍 THE WORLD

### The Setting
**Noakhali District, Bangladesh. 2070.**
Thirty years of rising sea levels have drowned the southern delta. Millions live in waterlogged stilt-slums built on corrugated iron and bamboo boardwalks above flooded streets. Corporate drones hum overhead. Bengali neon signs flicker beside English corporate billboards. The people are poorer than the technology around them suggests. That gap is where AXIOM operates.

## 🧬 THE ANCIENT SIPHON

**Nature:** A 450-million-year-old primordial living biological organ of Earth itself.
Not alien. Not a mineral deposit. A massive subterranean bio-magnetic neural wellspring that has rested beneath the Bengal Delta for hundreds of millions of years, regulating monsoon cycles, soil fertility, and geomagnetic balance.

In 2055, AXIOM's thermal drills punctured its nervous system. The agonizing wound triggered the catastrophic floods. The Siphon awoke in pain and self-defense.

**The Three Manifestations:**
| Manifestation | Carrier | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| The Wounded Wrath | **Kael** | Absorbed the Siphon's agony & rage — raw kinetic mass, burning green veins |
| The Pure Life | **Surjo** | Born as an independent Plant entity — infinite bio-energy, golden-teal wings, gentle pacifism |
| The Corrupted Tumor | **The Siphon Herald** | Toxic corporate drill waste fused with dead matter into an eldritch nightmare |

## 🏛️ THE THREE FACTIONS

### 1. AXIOM CORPORATION
Neutral multinational. No flag. No ideology beyond extraction and return-on-investment. Functionally evil through bureaucratic efficiency. Their soldiers follow orders because the system makes sense on paper.

### 2. GOVERNOR MUJIB'S SOVEREIGN STATE
A direct descendant of Sheikh Mujibur Rahman who signed the corporate pact with AXIOM to build flood dikes and save millions. He governs with ironclad military order from an elevated throne. Possesses Darth Vader-tier unrevealed sovereign powers (*The Sovereign Pressure* — gravitational crushing aura). Defeated in Act IV but **survives** as a shadow kingmaker.

### 3. JAHANGIR BHAI'S UNDERWORLD
The Godfather of the Wet Bazaars. Former brother-in-arms with Governor Mujib turned bitter rival. Controls all contraband supply barges, clean water distribution, and black-market commerce across the flooded delta. Pure pragmatist—funds the resistance because AXIOM is bad for business.

## 📍 KEY LOCATIONS

### Noakhali Block (Act I)
Waterlogged stilt-slums of Noakhali District. Corrugated metal shanties on stilts over dark water. Neon Bengali signs. AXIOM military checkpoints with harsh blue searchlights.

### The Wet Bazaars (Act II)
Sprawling floating black markets built on hundreds of lashed wooden sampans, barges, and submerged highway overpasses. Neon-lit food stalls. Jahangir Bhai's luxury mahogany paddle-wheel flagship *The Sultana* anchors at the center.

### Sublevel 7: The Inqilab Cryo-Vault (Act II)
Subterranean military preservation bunker beneath Old Dhaka. Frost-coated steel walls. Rows of dead cryo-pods. Bahadur's single functioning Mark-IV pod glowing amber.

### The Biolume Mangroves (Act III)
Mutated Sundarbans swampland. Water glows with bioluminescent teal algae. Mangrove trees have crystalline glass-bark trunks. Radar-jamming bio-spores float in dense fog.

### The Broken Meghna Crossing (Act III)
A colossal 2-mile suspension bridge spanning a flooded canyon gorge. Snapped steel cables. Arena for the legendary Surjo vs. KPop Ninja gunslinger duel.

### The Archive of the Drowned Dawn (Act III)
Master Diganta's sanctuary. An ancient university library preserved inside a dry golden energy sphere underwater. Towering bookshelves, brass astrolabes, and a floating quantum gyroscope orb.

### The Dubai Quarter (Act IV)
Ultra-wealthy elevated corporate district. Sleek glass skyscrapers, magnetic monorails, holographic advertisements in Arabic and Bengali.

### The Grand Pavilion / Bongo Dreadnought (Act IV)
Governor Mujib's throne room. A vast glass-domed ballroom atop a floating armored presidential dreadnought. Polished marble floors, blood-red ceremonial carpets, panoramic storm views.

### The Siphon Core / Tectonic Chasm (Act V)
Five miles beneath the earth. A colossal subterranean cavern of ancient cracked rock and pulsing living bio-magnetic tissue with glowing teal energy rivers.

### Old Dhaka Core (Prequel: First Blood)
Silent, ash-sealed city behind colossal blast doors. Strictly monochrome black, white, and grey. Only color: piercing red drone optics.

### The Sky-Scrap (Prequel: First Blood)
Chaotic dieselpunk vertical shantytown suspended between ruined skyscraper peaks. Steel cables, floating zeppelins, warm orange sunset smog.

### The Glass Wastes (Spinoff: Surjo)
Post-apocalyptic desert of heat-fused shimmering green glass dunes. Massive solar-powered armored refugee train on elevated rail tracks.

## 👑 THE CANON FINALE: THE SOVEREIGN GUARDIAN

Kael plunges both hands into the wounded Siphon mantle, absorbing the full planetary power:
- **The Transfiguration:** Obsidian crystalline armor and celestial teal energy veins erupt across his body. *The Shard* fuses permanently into his right arm.
- **The Impenetrable Sovereign Veil:** A colossal bio-magnetic pulse permanently seals Bangladesh's borders with a storm veil of teal and golden auroras. AXIOM satellites explode. Foreign fleets are expelled forever.
- **The Epilogue:** Kael stands in eternal vigil over Noakhali at midnight. Governor Mujib raises a glass in grim respect. Jahangir Bhai exhales cigar smoke with a knowing grin.

**Kael has become the immortal, eternal Sovereign Guardian of the Iron Delta.**
