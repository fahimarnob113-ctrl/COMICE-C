## 🌍 WORLD BACKGROUND

### The Setting
**Noakhali District, Bangladesh. 2070.**
Thirty years of catastrophic climate flooding have drowned the southern delta. Millions survive in waterlogged stilt-slums—corrugated iron shacks on bamboo stilts above dark canal waters. Corporate drones hum overhead. Bengali neon signs flicker beside English corporate billboards. AXIOM Corporation runs the checkpoints, the power grid, and the extraction drills.

### The Siphon
A 450-million-year-old **primordial living biological organ of Earth itself**—not alien, not mineral. A massive subterranean bio-magnetic neural wellspring buried beneath the Bengal Delta. In 2055, AXIOM's deep-mantle thermal drills punctured its nervous system, triggering the catastrophic floods that drowned southern Bangladesh. The Siphon is neither good nor evil; it is raw planetary life, now awake in agony.

### The Three Factions
1. **AXIOM Corporation** — Neutral multinational. No flag. Functionally evil through bureaucratic efficiency.
2. **Governor Mujib's Sovereign State** — A Bangabandhu descendant who signed corporate pacts to save millions, ruling with ironclad military order and Darth Vader-tier sovereign powers.
3. **Jahangir Bhai's Underworld** — The Godfather of the Wet Bazaars, a former brother-in-arms turned bitter rival of Mujib, funding the resistance purely because AXIOM is bad for business.

## 👤 CHARACTERS

### KAEL (The Iron Hound — Protagonist)
An elite frontline sergeant whose wife and 5-year-old daughter drowned when AXIOM closed emergency floodgates to protect their drill platforms. His commanding officer—his closest mentor—was executed by firing squad. Kael absorbed raw Siphon energy in a subterranean bunker, broke his chains, seized a slab of blast-door steel (*The Shard*), and carved his way to freedom. He fights with the cold, methodical certainty of a man with nothing left to lose.

### SURAIYA (Hero Codename: Gilded Lotus — "The Diva")
The nation's biggest pre-war superstar performer turned frontline warrior. Mitsuri Kanroji energy—aggressively kind, fitness-obsessed, emotionally transparent. She calls Kael *"Kael-bhaiyaaa!"* and forces him to eat saffron-pistachio protein sandesh. She fights with a segmented razor-sharp golden ribbon whip-sword. Governor Mujib terrifies her on a primal level—she recognizes his crushing sovereign presence as something inhuman (*Muzan-tier dread*).

### KPOP NINJA (Real Name: Arman — "The Sapphire Marksman")
Born a twin into the elite Chowdhury Syndicate of legendary sharpshooters. Both brothers developed peerless god-tier marksmanship through different philosophies. When the clan disbanded in the floods, the twins parted ways under an unspoken pact to see who would become the greatest marksman alive. His twin brother is still out there—an unrevealed master gunman. Arman chose the rooftops, wielding a 7-foot anti-materiel sniper rifle with cocky brilliance, narrating his fights to an imaginary streaming audience (*"Chat, clip that!"*). Pure kinetic sniper. No supernatural powers—just inhuman reflexes, parkour, and jawdropping aim.

### ZINNIA (Dr. Zinnia Haque — "The Smiling Toxicologist")
Brilliant bio-organic chemist who led AXIOM's Lab-09. When her team discovered the Siphon was a living organism, AXIOM executed all six of her colleagues and burned the archives. Zinnia survived by dosing herself with a prototype suspended-animation neurotoxin, appearing dead. She methodically gathered her surviving notes, synthesized a bandolier of lethal compounds, and vowed to dismantle AXIOM piece by piece—with an unwavering, polite smile.

### SURJO (The Siphon Plant — "The Human Typhoon")
Not a human mutated by the Siphon. Surjo IS the Siphon—a sentient biological Plant entity born directly from the subterranean wellspring, like Trigun Stampede's Vash. He carries a 250,000,000-credit AXIOM bounty (*DEAD OR ALIVE*) but holds a sacred vow of non-lethal combat. His oversized crimson trench coat, chrome cybernetic left arm, and custom silver break-action magnum (*The Silver Starling*) define his silhouette. When pushed to his absolute limit, ethereal golden-teal Siphon energy wings erupt from his back.

### BAHADUR (The Ancient Soldier — "The Relic")
The finest close-combat soldier the Inqilab Force ever produced. In 2029, when AXIOM pressured the government to dissolve the battalion, Bahadur alone refused. He led a devastating guerrilla campaign, destroying six AXIOM sub-stations, before his own government sedated him and sealed him in a cryogenic pod beneath Old Dhaka. His decommissioning file read: *"Status: Preserved. Reason: Politically Inconvenient."* He slept for forty-one years. Jensen Ackles exact face and likeness.

### MASTER DIGANTA (The Quantum Sage — "The Infinite Horizon")
The wisest man alive in the IRONBLOOD universe. Exact noble likeness of Rabindranath Tagore. He resides in The Archive of the Drowned Dawn—a submerged ancient library preserved in a dry golden energy bubble underwater. He casts Doctor Strange-style spinning golden geometric mandalas that can dissolve kinetic projectiles and pacify rampaging mutants. All factions respect him. The only person Bahadur is completely quiet around.

### GOVERNOR MUJIB (The Sovereign Titan)
A direct 2070 descendant of Sheikh Mujibur Rahman, carrying his exact face and commanding likeness. When the floods threatened 30 million lives, he signed the corporate pact with AXIOM to build dikes and enforce order. He possesses unrevealed sovereign powers: sub-dermal titanium combat rig, explosive kinetic martial arts, and *The Sovereign Pressure*—a Darth Vader-tier gravitational crushing aura. Defeated in Act IV but **survives**, withdrawing to his bunker as a neutral shadow titan.

### JAHANGIR BHAI (The Godfather of the Wet Bazaars)
The undisputed sovereign of Bangladesh's flooded underworld. Tommy Vercetti meets Don Corleone—pinstripe three-piece suit, heavy black wool overcoat, fedora, Cuban cigars, gold signet rings, gold snub-nosed revolver (*"The Ledger"*). Thirty years ago, he and Mujib were close brothers-in-arms. Their historic split defined the modern world: Mujib chose the state, Jahangir chose the shadows. A pure pragmatist who funds the resistance only because AXIOM's automated checkpoints are strangling his trade routes.

### USTAD (Faruk Mia — Master of the Grid)
In 2058, Substation 4's turbine seized during a monsoon surge. 400 workers were trapped behind locked bulkheads. Faruk Mia—a Chief Electrician—manually broke the emergency turbine with his 36-inch pipe wrench, redirecting the flood through the drainage core and saving every single life. By morning, nobody called him Faruk anymore. He was **Ustad**—The Master.

### BORKOT (The Iron Brother)
A massive 9-foot decommissioned AXIOM military combat mech who broke his corporate programming to guard Kael. They communicate in grunts, nods, and seamless battlefield coordination.

### JAMAL (The Street Runner)
A young Bengali teenager, thin and athletic, with flashy frosted-tip hair and a neon streetwear jacket. Kael's runner, scout, and surrogate son. Kael treats him with gruff discipline but will take a tank shell before letting a hair on his head get harmed.

## 📖 THE 5-ACT CAMPAIGN

### ACT I: THE FLOODED BLOCK (Noakhali Uprising)
* Kael and Borkot breach an AXIOM pacification checkpoint in the monsoon-drenched stilt-slums.
* Ustad repairs Borkot in his dry-dock garage while Jamal feeds intel.
* Liberating workers from the Siphon extraction labor compound.
* **Boss: THE WARDEN** — Heavy Iron Collar enforcer with electrified shock-whip.

### ACT II: THE WET BAZAARS & THE ANCIENT SOLDIER
* Kael travels through the floating black markets to meet **Jahangir Bhai** aboard the *Sultana*.
* Jahangir supplies demolition charges and watergate passcodes in exchange for Kael sabotaging AXIOM's tax grid.
* Infiltrating Sublevel 7 to steal an energy capacitor. **BAHADUR IS THAWED FROM ICE.**
* **Boss: COMMANDANT VOSS** — AXIOM cyber-exoskeleton commander.

### ACT III: THE BIOLUME MANGROVES & THE SAGE
* **Suraiya** and **Zinnia** navigate glowing teal glass-bark mangrove swamps.
* **Surjo** and **KPop Ninja** stage an insane long-range sniper/ricochet tag-team defense against an AXIOM armored column on the Broken Meghna Bridge.
* Kael enters **The Archive of the Drowned Dawn** and meets **MASTER DIGANTA**, who heals his raging Siphon blood with golden mandalas and gives him the harmonic frequency key.

### ACT IV: THE THRONE OF TIDES
* Scaling the Dubai Quarter sky-spires. Ustad rigs ziplines, Arman provides overwatch, Suraiya infiltrates the ballroom.
* **Boss: GOVERNOR MUJIB (3-Phase Titan Battle)**
  * Phase 1 (State Decree): Orbital laser grids and Red Directorate guards.
  * Phase 2 (Iron Will): Sub-dermal titanium rig activation—explosive kinetic martial arts.
  * Phase 3 (Sovereign Pressure): Darth Vader gravitational crushing aura, floating marble debris.
* **Mujib survives**, warns Kael of the waking core, and withdraws to his bunker.

### ACT V: THE LIVING CORE — THE SOVEREIGN GUARDIAN (Canon Finale)
* The entire united crew descends into the subterranean mantle.
* **Final Boss: THE SIPHON HERALD** — A 50-foot eldritch titan of rock, drill steel, and corrupted bio-matter.
* Full crew synergy battle: Arman snipes weak points; Suraiya clears tendrils; Zinnia injects enzymes; Bahadur tanks with his shield; Surjo unleashes golden wings; Kael delivers the final cleave.
* **THE ASCENSION:** Kael plunges both hands into the raw Siphon mantle. Obsidian crystalline armor and celestial teal energy erupt across his body. *The Shard* fuses permanently into his right arm. A colossal bio-magnetic pulse erupts, permanently sealing Bangladesh's borders with a teal-and-golden aurora storm veil.
* **Kael becomes the immortal, eternal Sovereign Guardian of the Iron Delta.**
