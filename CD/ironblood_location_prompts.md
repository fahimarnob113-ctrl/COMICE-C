# IRONBLOOD: Location & Background Prompt Compendium

All major environments, biomes, and landmark background prompts for the IRONBLOOD universe, formatted ready to copy/paste into Midjourney, DALL-E, or ComicGen Studio.

---

## 🌧️ ACT I: NOAKHALI BLOCK (The Drowned Slums)

### 1. Noakhali Checkpoint (Night & Rain)
> `Background concept art, NOAKHALI BLOCK CHECKPOINT. A flooded cyberpunk stilt-slum in coastal Bangladesh at night in heavy monsoon rain. Dilapidated corrugated metal shacks on wooden stilts over dark water, illuminated by flickering neon signs in Bengali script. Heavy AXIOM military barricades with harsh blue searchlights and barbed wire blocking a flooded alley. 16-bit arcade pixel art, Metal Slug aesthetic, gritty, atmospheric, dark blue and vibrant neon green contrast, wide horizontal composition.`

### 2. Ustad’s Dry-Dock Garage
> `Background concept art, USTAD'S WORKSHOP. The interior of a rusted dry-dock garage built inside an abandoned cargo barge. Workbenches covered in analog electrical tools, cracked pipe wrenches, stripped wires, and makeshift solar-battery cells. A damaged military combat mech sits in the repair bay under warm tungsten work lamps. Monsoon rain pouring outside open bay doors. 16-bit arcade pixel art style, Capcom CPS2 aesthetic, warm amber lighting, industrial clutter.`

### 3. The Extraction Labor Compound
> `Background concept art, EXTRACTION COMPOUND. A heavily fortified industrial prison camp in a muddy floodplain. Massive iron fences, watchtowers with blinding white spotlights, and colossal pneumatic Siphon mining drills piercing the earth, leaking faint teal energy vapor into the rain. Cold, oppressive, industrial dystopia, 16-bit arcade pixel art style, high contrast, gritty realism.`

## 🚤 ACT II: THE WET BAZAARS & CRYO-VAULTS

### 4. The Wet Bazaars (Floating Market)
> `Background concept art, THE WET BAZAARS. A sprawling, neon-lit floating night market built across hundreds of wooden sampans, barges, and submerged highway overpasses in a flooded city. Street food vendors with smoking woks under hanging paper lanterns, glowing violet and emerald neon signs reflected on the dark rippling water. Dense, chaotic, vibrant cyberpunk marketplace, 16-bit arcade pixel art style, Metal Slug aesthetic, rich colors.`

### 5. The Sultana (Jahangir Bhai’s Luxury Barge Interior)
> `Background concept art, JAHANGIR BHAI'S STATE ROOM. Interior of a luxurious three-story vintage paddle-wheel river barge. Rich dark mahogany wood paneled walls, stained glass windows with rain streaming down, antique Persian rugs, crystal amber chandeliers, and a carved teakwood desk with a smoking cigar in an ashtray. Classic mobster aesthetic meets cyberpunk, 16-bit arcade pixel art style, cinematic noir lighting, warm golden tones.`

### 6. Sublevel 7: The Inqilab Cryo-Vault
> `Background concept art, SUBLEVEL 7 CRYO VAULT. A subterranean abandoned military preservation bunker beneath flooded ruins. Cold steel walls covered in thick frost and icicles, rows of dead preservation pods, and a single functioning Mark-IV cryogenic chamber glowing with amber emergency warning lights. Steam and frozen condensation rolling across the grated floor. 16-bit arcade pixel art style, sci-fi thriller aesthetic, eerie cold blue and amber contrast.`

## 🌿 ACT III: THE BIOLUME SWAMPS & THE ARCHIVE

### 7. The Glass-Bark Mangroves (Sundarbans)
> `Background concept art, THE BIOLUME MANGROVES. A mysterious flooded mangrove swamp in the Sundarbans at night. The water glows with intense bioluminescent teal algae. Mangrove trees have crystalline glass-like bark that shimmers in the dark, with twisted roots forming natural arches. Dense mystical fog, floating bio-spores, eerie and ethereal, 16-bit arcade pixel art style, glowing teal and deep indigo palette, magical sci-fi atmosphere.`

### 8. The Broken Meghna Bridge (Gunslinger Duel Arena)
> `Background concept art, THE BROKEN MEGHNA CROSSING. A colossal, decaying suspension bridge spanning miles over a flooded canyon gorge under a blistering midday sun. Snapped steel cables hanging like giant vines, rusted vehicle chassis scattered across cracked asphalt, heat mirage rising from the road, deep canyon mist below. 16-bit arcade pixel art style, wide panoramic view, cinematic western cyberpunk aesthetic.`

### 9. The Archive of the Drowned Dawn (Master Diganta’s Sanctuary)
> `Background concept art, THE DROWNED ARCHIVE. An ancient submerged university library preserved inside a dry, airtight golden energy sphere underwater. Water and swimming fish visible through the transparent ceiling dome. Inside, towering bookshelves filled with millions of ancient books, astronomical brass astrolabes, and a floating quantum gyroscope orb casting warm golden light. 16-bit arcade pixel art style, majestic, peaceful, golden sanctuary, rich detail.`

## 🧬 ACT V: THE TECTONIC CORE & THE FINALE

### 12. The Tectonic Chasm (The Siphon Core)
> `Background concept art, THE SIPHON CORE. A colossal subterranean cavern five miles beneath the earth. The walls are a terrifying fusion of ancient cracked rock and pulsing, living bio-magnetic organic tissue with glowing teal energy rivers flowing into an abyss. Massive broken corporate drill bits suspended from the ceiling. Eldritch, planetary scale, 16-bit arcade pixel art style, dark obsidian rock and vibrant glowing teal energy.`

### 13. The Sovereign Overlook (Midnight Epilogue)
> `Background concept art, THE SOVEREIGN OVERLOOK. A high stone cliff outcrop overlooking the entire flooded Bengal Delta at midnight. In the sky above, a colossal storm veil of glowing teal and golden auroras seals the atmosphere. Below, the distant neon lights of Noakhali Block flicker peacefully in the rain. Epic, cinematic final shot, 16-bit arcade pixel art style, breathtaking scale, serene dark fantasy cyberpunk.`

## 🏜️ PREQUEL & SPINOFF SPECIAL LOCATIONS

The entire environment is strictly monochrome black, white, and grey. > `Background concept art, THE SKY-SCRAP. A chaotic dieselpunk vertical shantytown suspended thousands of feet in the air between ruined skyscraper peaks. Lashed together with heavy steel cables, rusted scrap platforms, and floating zeppelins. Warm smoggy orange sunset sky, spinning propellers, howling wind, flickering barrel fires. 16-bit arcade pixel art style, dynamic vertical composition, warm orange and bronze palette.`
