# 1. CORE VISUAL DNA & INFLUENCES

The visual identity of **IRONBLOOD** is built on a high-octane fusion of three distinct eras of art:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. LATE 90s ARCADE PIXEL ART (Capcom CPS2 / Neo-Geo Metal Slug)             │
│    Chunky silhouettes, expressive muscle anatomy, hard pixel contours.      │
│                                                                             │
│ 2. DARK SEINEN CYBERPUNK (Katsuhiro Otomo's AKIRA / Ghost in the Shell)     │
│    Dense industrial clutter, weathered materials, atmospheric rain/smoke.   │
│                                                                             │
│ 3. YOJI SHINKAWA & SHINKIRO CHARACTER DESIGN                               │
│    Dramatic high-contrast ink shading, sharp facial planes, regal presence. │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Core Philosophy:
* **"Tactile, Gritty, and Heavy"**: Nothing looks clean, glossy, or weightless. Armor is dented, boots are caked in dark mud, weapons show grinding marks, and concrete has water stains.
* **"Neon on the Surface ➔ Progressively Darker Underground"**: The surface world is an electric clash of Bengali neon and rain; the underground is a bioluminescent, ancient abyss.

# 2. LINEWORK, CONTOURS & SHADING RULES

### 1. Hard Ink Contours (Strict Rule)
* Every character and prominent object must have a **bold, readable, 1px dark border/ink contour** (`#07070e`).
* **NO smooth airbrushing** or soft gradient falloffs inside character silhouettes.
* Outer silhouettes must be distinct and immediately recognizable in pure solid black.

### 2. Cel-Shaded 3D Directional Shading
* **Primary Key Light:** Crisp highlight along the top-left edges of anatomical planes, armor plates, and blade edges.
* **Core Shadow:** Deep, solid-fill dark tone along the bottom-right surfaces.
* **Volumetric Rim Light:** A sharp, narrow emissive glow (Siphon Green, AXIOM Cyan, or Amber) tracing the outer rim of the character opposite the key light.

### 3. Texture & Material Rendering
* **Steel & Iron:** Chunky edge-highlights with cross-hatched scuffs and rust stains.
* **Skin:** Warm, weathered, scarred South Asian skin tones with crisp planar shadow cutoffs.
* **Energy / Siphon Glow:** Pure, unadulterated high-luminance emission (Emerald Green `#00e87a` / Celestial Teal `#00d4aa`) with sharp volumetric light spill across nearby surfaces.

# 5. CINEMATOGRAPHY & SAKUGA ACTION FRAMING

When generating manga panels or combat concept art, always enforce these cinematic rules:

### 1. Dynamic Extreme Angles
* **Low-Angle Hero Tilt:** Camera placed at ankle-level looking up, exaggerating shoulder breadth, weapon length, and towering mechs.
* **High-Speed Dutch Tilt:** 15° to 30° diagonal horizon tilt during sprint, slide, and strike panels to convey kinetic velocity.

### 2. Foreshortening & Kinetic Energy
* Thrusts, punches, and sword swings must break the panel border with heavy perspective foreshortening (e.g. cleaver blade massive in foreground, character torso smaller in background).
* **Speedlines & Debris:** Rain streaks, flying concrete fragments, hydraulic steam vents, and glowing energy embers must follow the motion vectors.

### 3. The 3-Layer Depth Standard
* **Layer 1 (Foreground):** Dynamic element (swinging cleaver, shattered glass, muzzle flash).
* **Layer 2 (Midground):** Main characters in combat action with full expression and pose.
* **Layer 3 (Background):** Flooded cyberpunk skyline, atmospheric fog, searchlights.

# 8. MODEL-SPECIFIC EXECUTION DIRECTIVES

### 🔹 Midjourney (v6 / v6.1) Directives
* **Aspect Ratios:**
  * Portraits / Single Character: `--ar 2:3`
  * Action Panels / Environments: `--ar 16:9`
  * Splash / Climax Pages: `--ar 9:16` or `--ar 21:9`
* **Consistency Flags:**
  * Use `--cref [URL]` with `--cw 100` to lock character facial features and tactical costumes.
  * Set `--stylize 250` for crisp line rendering.

### 🔹 Flux.1 / Flux.1 Kontext Directives
* Place the core character anchor keywords at the **very beginning** of the prompt string.
* Use explicit natural language for lighting: *"lit by harsh directional emerald neon light from the left, casting sharp deep black shadows to the right"*.

### 🔹 Google Imagen 3 / Google Flow Directives
* Use the **"Frames & Ingredients"** entity system to attach locked character concept art images as reference anchors.
* Specify: *"Retro 16-bit arcade pixel art style with hard black outlines, CPS2 arcade aesthetic, high contrast."*
