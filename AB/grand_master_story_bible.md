# IRONBLOOD: GRAND MASTER STORY BIBLE (Universe Compendium)

**Title:** IRONBLOOD  
**Genre:** Cyberpunk Delta-Punk / Seinen Dark Fantasy / 16-Bit Arcade Beat-Em-Up  
**Setting:** Drowned Post-Collapse Bangladesh, 2029–2070  

---

## ⏳ 1. THE COMPLETE UNIVERSE TIMELINE

```
2025–2029: THE FINAL SOVEREIGN ERA
├── Inqilab Force operates as Bangladesh's apex sovereign black-ops unit.
└── 2029: Corporate treaties signed; BAHADUR refuses to disband, leads guerrilla campaign, 
    and is secretly sealed in cryogenic stasis beneath Old Dhaka as "Politically Inconvenient."

2055–2058: THE GREAT TECTONIC FLOOD
├── AXIOM drills into the subterranean mantle, puncturing the living planetary Siphon.
├── The agonizing wound triggers tectonic sea-level floods, drowning southern Bangladesh.
├── 2058: Substation 4 flood disaster; USTAD manually breaks the turbine to save 400 workers.
└── GOVERNOR MUJIB signs the Pact of Sovereignty with AXIOM to build dikes and save millions.

2067: THE ORIGIN HEIST ("FIRST BLOOD")
├── Anonymous broker JAHANGIR BHAI hires 6 specialists for the Old Dhaka server heist.
├── Kael, Jamal, Ustad, Suraiya, Zinnia, and KPop Ninja collide in the monochrome ash.
└── They destroy an AXIOM Hunter Gunship, escape on a zeppelin, and form the resistance.

2070: THE 5-ACT MAIN CAMPAIGN & SPINOFFS
├── Act I: Noakhali Block Uprising; Kael & Borkot defeat The Warden.
├── Act II: Kael cuts deal with Jahangir on his barge; BAHADUR IS THAWED FROM ICE.
├── Act III: Biolume Mangroves; Surjo & KPop Ninja sniper flank; MASTER DIGANTA'S ARCHIVE.
├── Act IV: Dubai Quarter Sky-Spire climb; GOVERNOR MUJIB TITAN BATTLE (Mujib survives).
├── Act V: Descent into the mantle; Siphon Herald destroyed; KAEL ASCENDS AS SOVEREIGN GUARDIAN.
└── Spinoff: SURJO: OUTLAW OF THE ASH (Protecting the refugee train across the badlands).
```

## 👥 2. THE 10-CHARACTER PANTHEON & PROMPTS

### 1. KAEL (The Iron Hound — Protagonist)
* **Archetype:** Wolverine / Frank Castle (Punisher) haunted soldier. Lost his wife, 5-year-old daughter, and commanding officer to AXIOM.
* **Weapon:** *The Shard* (colossal jagged cleaver sword resonating with Siphon energy).
* **Prompt:** `Character concept art, KAEL. A rugged, muscular South Asian combat veteran in his mid-30s with scarred dark skin, haunted piercing eyes, thick stubble. Pulsing neon green Siphon energy veins glow on left arm and chest. Dark charcoal tactical fatigue armor with combat straps and heavy boots. Resting a colossal jagged iron cleaver sword with glowing green energy fissures on his shoulder. Gritty, ferocious, Frank Castle meets Wolverine, 16-bit arcade pixel art style, Capcom CPS2 aesthetic, high contrast.`

## 🌍 3. THE WORLDBUILDING & MYTHOS

1. **The Ancient Siphon:** A 450-million-year-old living subterranean planetary organ of Earth that regulated the tides. Punctured by AXIOM's thermal drills in 2055, triggering the floods.
2. **The Wet Bazaars:** Sprawling floating neon black markets built on thousands of lashed sampans, barges, and flooded highway overpasses, ruled by Jahangir Bhai.
3. **The Biolume Mangroves:** Mutated radar-jamming glass-bark mangroves and glowing teal algae canals in the Sundarbans.
4. **Old Dhaka Core:** An eerie, monochrome ash-sealed city patrolled by red-eyed automated drones.
5. **The Sky-Scrap:** A roaring, orange dieselpunk vertical shantytown lashed between skyscraper peaks and zeppelins.

## 👑 4. THE CANON FINALE: THE SOVEREIGN GUARDIAN

In Act V, Kael plunges both hands into the wounded Siphon mantle, absorbing its full planetary power:
- An **impenetrable bio-magnetic storm veil** permanently seals Bangladesh's borders from the outside world.
- AXIOM's satellites and foreign armadas are permanently expelled.
- Kael ascends as the immortal **Sovereign Guardian of the Iron Delta**, standing silent vigil over Noakhali at midnight with eternal teal-glowing eyes.
