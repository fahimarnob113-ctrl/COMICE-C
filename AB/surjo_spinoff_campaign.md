# SURJO: OUTLAW OF THE ASH — Spinoff Campaign Bible

**Protagonist:** Surjo (*The Siphon Plant / The Human Typhoon*)  
**Bounty:** 250,000,000 AXIOM Credits (*DEAD OR ALIVE*)  
**Format:** 3-Act Standalone Action-Adventure Campaign  
**Vibe:** Trigun Stampede 2024 meets Cyberpunk Mad Max  

---

## 📖 CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW

While Kael fights in the urban checkpoints of Noakhali, the outer badlands are a brutal, lawless wasteland of sun-scorched glass dunes, flooded ghost towns, and roaming mercenary syndicates.

**The Hook:** Surjo stumbles upon **The Ark of Meghna**—a massive, battered solar-powered refugee train carrying 800 displaced children and families trying to reach the sovereign safe zone in the north. 

When AXIOM's elite Bounty Guild and desert warlords realize the 250M-credit "Human Typhoon" is riding aboard the train, every hired gun in the badlands attacks. Surjo must defend the train across 1,000 miles of hostile territory—**without killing a single attacker.**

---

## 🗺️ ACT-BY-ACT BREAKDOWN

### ACT I: THE GLASS WASTES
* **Setting:** A vast desert of heat-fused green glass sand where pre-Collapse cities melted. Blinding sun, heat mirages, roaring sandstorms.
* **Mission 1 (The Roadhouse Duel):** Surjo is quietly eating flatbread in a dusty glass roadhouse when a squad of 20 cyber-bounty hunters kicks down the doors. A chaotic, acrobatic gun-kata bar fight where Surjo disarms everyone using ceiling fans and soup bowls.
* **Mission 2 (The Train Ambush):** The Ark of Meghna is intercepted by motorized dune-buggy raiders armed with harpoons and magnetic mines. Surjo rides on top of the moving train cars, shooting harpoon cables out of the air.
* **Boss: CRUSHER KAZI** — A cybernetic desert warlord driving a six-wheeled spiked battle-tank equipped with twin flamethrowers. Surjo defeats him by ricocheting a single kinetic slug into the tank's cooling manifold, stalling the engine harmlessly.

---

### ACT II: THE FLOODED CANYONS
* **Setting:** Deep limestone gorges flooded with turbulent teal-green river rapids and submerged suspension bridges.
* **Mission 3 (The Canyon Run):** The train travels along precarious cliffside rail tracks while aerial drone swarms dive-bomb the canyon.
* **Mission 4 (The Sniper on the Span):** A high-altitude sniper duel along a 3-mile broken rail bridge. (Guest appearance by **KPop Ninja**, where their legendary rivalry first blossoms into mutual respect!).
* **Boss: VALKYRIE VEX** — A mercenary ace pilot in a prototype VTOL strike jet. Surjo grapples onto the jet's landing gear mid-flight, unplugs her weapons battery, and glides back down using his crimson coat as a parachute.

---

### ACT III: THE SIEGE OF BORDER ZERO
* **Setting:** The massive electrified border wall separating the lawless badlands from the sovereign northern sanctuaries.
* **Mission 5 (The Final Stand):** An entire AXIOM armored column blocks the border gates. With the train out of fuel, Surjo stands alone on the tracks in front of the refugees, facing three armored walkers and a hundred troops.
* **Final Boss: COMMANDER DRAKE & THE COLOSSUS DREADNOUGHT** — A walking 4-story fortress mech.
* **The Climax:** Surjo is pushed to his absolute physical limit. To protect the children behind him, his **golden-teal Siphon wings** violently burst into existence for the first time. Radiating pure bio-kinetic energy, he fires one concentrated non-lethal micro-gravitational pulse that permanently fries the Colossus's reactor core, opening the border gates.

---

## 🌅 EPILOGUE: THE WANDERING SUN

The refugees safely cross the border into freedom, cheering Surjo's name.

Surjo doesn't cross. He turns around, picks up his dusty travel bag, puts his yellow tactical glasses back on, and walks back into the scorched badlands alone, grinning:

> *"Well... back to finding a decent cup of chai."*
