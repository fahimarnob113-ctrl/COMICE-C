# IRONBLOOD: USTAD — The Master's Origin

**Title:** *The Wrench That Built the Underground*

---

## 🔧 THE BACKSTORY: BEFORE THE NAME

Before he was **Ustad** ("The Master"), his name was **Faruk Mia**. 

Thirty years ago, Faruk was the Chief Master Electrician for the **Old Dhaka Municipal Grid & Flood Pumphouse #4**. He was the man who kept the lights on when the monsoons hit, hand-calibrating massive analog turbines in knee-deep water. He wore the standard-issue yellow city worker's helmet that he still wears to this day.

When AXIOM Corporation arrived with their private security contracts, they installed automated proprietary power nodes and fired the municipal crews. Faruk warned the corporate overseers that their high-voltage extraction conduits would overload the district's aging drainage pumps during the flood season.

The AXIOM suits ignored him.

---

## ⚡ THE DISASTER: NIGHT OF THE BLOWN GATES

In the monsoon of 2058, AXIOM's conduit spiked. The automated grid suffered a catastrophic short circuit, and the emergency floodgates jammed shut. 

While corporate managers evacuated by rooftop gunships, Faruk refused to leave. He dove into the flooded high-voltage turbine chamber with nothing but a heavy pipe wrench and insulated gloves, manually breaking the turbine driveshaft to release the trapped water pressure and prevent a city-wide electrical explosion.

The mechanical back-torque shattered his helmet visor, fractured his collarbone, and permanently scarred his arms. But he saved four hundred workers trapped in the lower sublevels.

---

## 🛠️ THE BIRTH OF "USTAD"

When Faruk walked out of the wreckage, the city was changed. AXIOM sealed the Old Dhaka Core behind blast doors. The displaced working-class poor were pushed onto the waterlogged stilts of Noakhali Block.

Faruk never left his people. He set up an open-air workshop in an abandoned dry-dock. 
- He taught runaway kids (like young Jamal) how to identify live AXIOM high-voltage conduits.
- He scavenged corporate scrap to build decentralized solar-mesh microgrids for slum hospitals and kitchens.
- Whenever an AXIOM blast door or security gate stood in the resistance's way, they didn't bring explosives—they called Faruk.

The locals stopped calling him Faruk. Out of genuine reverence, everyone—from street kids to hardened mercenaries—began calling him **Ustad** (The Master). 

---

## 🎯 HIS SIGNATURE MOTTO & ITEMS

- **The Cracked Yellow Hard-Hat:** The exact municipal helmet from the 2058 flood. He refuses to replace it: *"If I take it off, I forget what happens when suits do engineering."*
- **The 36-Inch Cast-Iron Pipe Wrench:** Both his primary tool and a devastating blunt weapon.
- **The Core Rule:** *"If it has wires, I can make it explode. If it doesn't... give me a minute."*
