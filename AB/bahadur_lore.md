# IRONBLOOD: BAHADUR — The Ancient Soldier

**Name:** **BAHADUR** ("The Brave" / *The Relic*)  
**Inspiration:** Soldier Boy (*The Boys*) — Jensen Ackles Face & Build  
**Status:** Cryogenic Super-Soldier, Thawed in 2070 After 40 Years on Ice  
**Affiliation:** The Inqilab Force (Dissolved 2029) / The Resistance (2070)  

---

## 📜 1. ORIGIN: THE LAST VANGUARD

### Born in the Final Golden Age
Before the floods. Before AXIOM. Before the Siphon tore the earth open. Bangladesh had its own sovereign military apex: **The Inqilab Force**—an elite black-ops battalion engineered to protect the nation's sovereignty from corporate and foreign military encroachment.

Bahadur was their crown jewel.

At 28 years old, he was the finest close-combat soldier the Vanguard ever produced—biologically enhanced with dense ballistic musculature, reinforced skeletal plating woven from pre-Collapse composite alloys, and a sub-dermal radiogenic core implanted at the base of his sternum. 

His face was the face of victory—rugged, chiseled jaw, thick dark beard, cold hazel eyes that never blinked in firefights. The papers called him *"Bahadur"*—the brave one—after he dismantled four corporate armored walkers with nothing but his bronze heater shield and a serrated trench knife during the Khulna Border Crisis of 2025.

---

### The Betrayal & The Ice
In 2029, as AXIOM Corporation signed its first infrastructure treaty with Bangladesh's government, it quietly pressured for the dissolution of the Inqilab Force—a sovereign military force that posed a direct threat to their expanding operations.

Bahadur refused to disband. He led a final, devastating guerrilla campaign against AXIOM's first extraction facilities—alone, outnumbered, and outgunned—destroying six corporate sub-stations and three supply convoys before his own government betrayed him.

Rather than imprisoning or executing their finest soldier publicly, the coalition quietly sedated Bahadur and sealed him in a **Mark-IV Cryogenic Preservation Pod** in the deepest vault beneath the Old Dhaka Grid Authority building.

His decommissioning file read: *"Status: Preserved. Reason: Politically Inconvenient."*

He slept for **forty-one years**.

## ❄️ 2. THE AWAKENING SCENE: *"THE THAW"*

**Location:** Old Dhaka Core, Sublevel 7, Grid Authority Vault — 2070

**PANEL 1:**
Kael and Ustad breach Sublevel 7 looking for an ancient AXIOM power junction. Instead, they find an entire cryo-vault.

**USTAD:**
What... is this?

**PANEL 2:**
A massive chamber. Row upon row of preservation pods, all dead and dark except one—a single, frost-encrusted Mark-IV pod in the far corner, its emergency power cell still blinking amber.

**PANEL 3:**
Ustad plugs into the pod's terminal. His eyes go wide.

**USTAD:**
The Inqilab Force? These files are from 2029! This man has been sealed down here for... forty-one years!

**PANEL 4:**
Kael steps closer, looking through the frost-covered viewport.

The face inside is rugged, handsome, dark-bearded, and utterly still.

**KAEL:**
...Open it.

**PANEL 5 (Full Page):**
The pod releases with a thunderous hydraulic hiss. Steam and frozen condensation pour across the vault floor like a rolling fog. A massive silhouette sits up from the cryo-pod, cracking thick ice off his shoulders with the sound of splitting stone.

**BAHADUR:**
*(Groaning, disoriented, blinking his cold hazel eyes in the dim light)*  
*"...What smells like burning plastic?"*

## 💥 3. THE CULTURE SHOCK: 41 YEARS FORWARD

The moment Bahadur emerges from the Old Dhaka vault and sees the 2070 world above ground is played entirely for both **comedy and genuine heartbreak**:

**On Drones:**
> *[An AXIOM security drone flies overhead]*  
> **BAHADUR:** *(Dead serious)* "Is that a model plane?"  
> **JAMAL:** "It's a combat drone, Bahadur bhai. It shoots missiles."  
> **BAHADUR:** "A what. Who thought THAT was a good idea?!"

**On AXIOM Soldiers:**
> *[A squad of AXIOM troops in sleek polymer armor*]  
> **BAHADUR:** "Why is everyone wearing bathroom tiles for armor?"  
> **KPOP NINJA:** "It's polymer nano-composite ballistic—"  
> **BAHADUR:** *(Already walking toward them with his bronze shield)* "Doesn't matter. Looks breakable."

**On KPop Ninja's Blindfold:**
> **BAHADUR:** "Son, why are you fighting with a cloth over your eyes?"  
> **KPOP NINJA:** "I see better without looking."  
> **BAHADUR:** *(Long pause)* "...This country has lost its mind while I was asleep."

**On Discovering His Cryo-File:**
> **BAHADUR:** *(Reading the file: "Status: Politically Inconvenient")* *(Long silence)*  
> *"...Politically inconvenient."*  
> *(He folds the paper very carefully, puts it in his chest pocket, and says nothing more about it for the rest of the day)*

## ⚔️ 4. COMBAT STYLE & ARSENAL

- **The Bronze Shield (*"Rokkhoshotto"* / The Iron Guard):** A dense, battle-worn pointed bronze and steel heater shield that has deflected tank rounds, high-velocity railgun slugs, and orbital laser strikes. He wields it with both aggressive close-quarters offense and impenetrable defense.
- **The Serrated Trench Knife:** A long, thick-bladed pre-Collapse combat knife worn in a calf sheath. Old-fashioned, devastatingly effective.
- **Radiogenic Core (The Chest Blast):** When physical damage exceeds his bio-threshold, the sub-dermal radiogenic core at his sternum enters critical overcharge. He can direct or release this as a **catastrophic radioactive-kinetic shockwave**, vaporizing armored mechs in a 50-meter radius. The cost: each use visibly ages him for a few hours—haggard, pale—before his enhanced biology recovers.

## 💬 5. RELATIONSHIPS WITH THE CREW

### With KAEL:
Two broken, unbending soldiers from different eras who immediately respect each other without saying it.
> **BAHADUR:** *(Watching Kael fight an AXIOM mech with his cleaver)*  
> *"Reckless. Angry. No footwork. Reminds me of me at twenty-five."*  
> **KAEL:** "I can hear you."  
> **BAHADUR:** "Good. Fix your footwork."

### With USTAD:
Complete and genuine mutual respect. Two working-class craftsmen from different generations.
> **BAHADUR:** *(Watching Ustad rewire an entire AXIOM power junction in six minutes flat)*  
> *"I've seen Army engineers take four hours to do what you just did with a cracked wrench and a gamcha."*  
> **USTAD:** *(Grinning)* "Those Army engineers weren't from Noakhali."

### With JAMAL:
Bahadur is deeply uncomfortable with how young Jamal is in a war zone but would die before admitting he sees him like a son.
> **BAHADUR:** *(After Jamal does something impossibly reckless and survives)*  
> *"If you ever do that again, I will personally put you back in a cryo-pod until you're thirty."*  
> **JAMAL:** *(Grinning)* "You'd have to catch me first, Bhai!"  
> **BAHADUR:** *(Fighting a smile)* "...Don't push it."

### With MASTER DIGANTA:
The only person Bahadur is genuinely, completely quiet around. He sits in Diganta's archive without foul commentary or complaints, listening like a humbled student.
> **MASTER DIGANTA:** *(Handing him a cup of tea)*  
> *"Forty-one years is a long sleep, Bahadur. How do you feel?"*  
> **BAHADUR:** *(Long pause. Honest, quiet for once)*  
> *"...Like the world moved on without asking me."*  
> **MASTER DIGANTA:** *(Softly)* "It always does. That is why the brave choose to catch up."
