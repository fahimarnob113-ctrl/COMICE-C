## 1. 📜 MASTER DIGANTA (The Quantum Sage — Inspired by Rabindranath Tagore)
**Name:** **MASTER DIGANTA** ("The Infinite Horizon" / The Archon of the Drowned Archive)

### Concept & Lore:
A legendary cyber-philosopher, quantum polymath, and cosmic sage from the pre-Collapse universities. He views the Siphon not as a corporate fuel tank or an alien monster, but as an ancient cosmic poem and acoustic frequency that humanity is too crude to understand. 

He refuses all conventional firearms. Instead, he channels quantum acoustic frequencies and code-runes through his gyroscopic staff, bending kinetic energy, projecting shimmering harmonic barriers that deflect orbital bombardments, and disarming strike teams with resonant soundwaves.

### Visual & Outfit Design:
- **Face & Hair:** Long flowing silver-white hair and a majestic, immaculate silver beard. Deep, serene, all-knowing dark eyes. Exact noble, contemplative Tagore likeness.
- **Attire:** A flowing, ankle-length midnight-blue and cream ceremonial *angrakha* robe woven with subtle fiber-optic circuitry that illuminates in gold fractal scripts when he channels energy. 
- **Weapon/Focus:** A polished dark-wood walking staff topped with a floating, gyroscopic quantum orb.
- **Combat Style:** Harmonic zone control, kinetic barrier projection, gravity distortion, and sonic resonance blasts.

### Defining Lines:
- *"You try to conquer the Siphon with bullets and drills. You cannot conquer a melody with a hammer, child."*
- *"The flood was not an ending. It was simply the world turning a new page."*
- *[Taps staff on the stone floor, instantly dissolving a salvo of AXIOM laser fire into harmless golden mist]* *"Be at peace."*

## 2. ⚡ GOVERNOR MUJIB (The Dictator)
**Name:** **GOVERNOR MUJIB**

### Concept & Lore:
The iron-fisted supreme Governor of the eastern megacity coalition who signed the original development pact with AXIOM Corporation. He believes ruthless centralized order is the only thing keeping millions of citizens from drowning in total post-Collapse chaos. A terrifyingly charismatic orator whose booming voice over city-wide broadcast towers can pacify or mobilize entire armies.

### Visual & Outfit Design:
- **Face & Posture:** Imposing, broad-shouldered, towering presence. Iconic thick black-framed retro glasses, sharp slicked-back greying hair, authoritative mustache.
- **Attire:** A tailored, pitch-black *Mujib coat* with a high Mandarin collar, adorned with crimson AXIOM command crests and gold shoulder aiguillettes, worn over a crisp white formal tunic and dark trousers. Smoking a vintage cyber-pipe with glowing neon-amber embers.
- **Weapon/Command:** Commands orbital laser strikes, autonomous iron dreadnoughts, and carries a personalized gold-plated heavy revolver at his hip.

## 3. 🔴 SURJO (The Wanderer — Inspired by Vash *Trigun Stampede 2024*)
**Name:** **SURJO** ("The Sun")

### Concept & Lore:
A legendary wandering gunslinger with a catastrophic AXIOM bounty on his head who travels the ruined outskirts of Bangladesh. He is an absolute pacifist at heart, desperately trying to de-escalate conflicts and protect innocents, but possesses god-tier kinetic reflexes. Catastrophic collateral damage seems to follow him wherever he walks. *(Primed for his own standalone spinoff campaign!)*

### Visual & Outfit Design:
- **Face & Hair:** Asymmetrical messy dirty-blonde/light brown hair with an undercut. Light amber/yellow tinted tactical shooting glasses. Warm, tired, expressive smile hiding deep scars.
- **Attire:** An oversized, asymmetrical **vibrant crimson-red techwear trench coat/bomber jacket** with a high structured collar, trailing straps, and weathered hem. Black tactical undersuit with reinforced knee pads and worn desert combat boots.
- **Prosthetic Arm:** His entire left arm is a sleek, white-and-steel cybernetic prosthetic with exposed blue power conduit lines.
- **Weapon:** A massive, custom break-action magnum revolver (*The Silver Starling*) firing hyper-dense kinetic rounds.

## 4. 🛡️ BAHADUR (The Relic — Inspired by Soldier Boy)
**Name:** **BAHADUR** ("The Brave" / The Ancient Soldier)

### Concept & Lore:
A legendary, biologically augmented super-soldier from an early pre-Collapse black-ops battalion (The Inqilab Force). Locked in cryogenic stasis in a subterranean bunker beneath Old Dhaka for over forty years, he was recently thawed out into a ruined, climate-changed 2070 world he despises. 

He is cynical, foul-mouthed, and completely unapologetic about his outdated 20th-century machismo. He hates AXIOM's modern automated drones, calling their pilots "cowards hiding behind tablets," and prefers bashing combat mechs into scrap with his bare fists and heavy bronze battle-shield.

### Visual & Outfit Design:
- **Face & Hair:** Pure Jensen Ackles aesthetic—ruggedly handsome, chiseled square jaw, thick dark beard/stubble, disheveled dark brown military hair, and piercing, cold weathered hazel eyes. Unapologetic vintage 80s action hero presence.
- **Attire:** A weathered **dark forest-green and bronze scaled ballistic supersuit** with dark leather tactical straps, combat pouches, heavy military combat boots, and an eagle/starburst crest etched into his reinforced chest plate. 
- **Weapon:** A heavy, pointed **bronze-and-steel ballistic heater shield** emblazoned with a battle-worn eagle emblem, used for blunt concussive bashes and deflecting high-caliber artillery. Carries a vintage serrated trench knife at his boot.
- **Ultimate Ability (Chest Blast):** When pushed beyond his limits, his chest core overcharges with a catastrophic radioactive-kinetic shockwave that vaporizes enemy armor and fries all electronic grids in a 50-meter radius.

### Defining Lines:
- *"I didn't survive forty years on ice to get bossed around by kids in spandex and Wi-Fi drones."*
- *"Back in my day, if you had a problem with someone, you beat them with your hands. Not a fucking joystick."*
- *[Coughs, lights an antique cigar amid a pile of burning AXIOM mechs]* *"Is that all you got?"*

## ⚡ 5. THE SIPHON "AWAKENING" MECHANIC

Prolonged proximity to the ancient Siphon deep beneath Bangladesh triggers unique, irreversible genetic and kinetic mutations in certain individuals:

- **Kael:** *The Siphon Berserk* — Teal energy veins ignite, amplifying physical mass, raw sword-cleaving power, and kinetic inertia.
- **Surjo:** *Hyper-Kinetic Perception* — The nervous system accelerates, seeing ballistic trajectories in slow motion and enabling impossible ricochet marksmanship.
- **Bahadur:** *Core Meltdown* — His pre-Collapse bio-enhancements react with the Siphon's radiation, converting physical damage taken into a devastating radioactive shockwave blast.
- **The Siphon Herald:** *Organic Bio-Fusion* — Complete surrender to the Siphon, fusing ancient earth, metal, and absorbed consciousness into a shifting eldritch entity.
