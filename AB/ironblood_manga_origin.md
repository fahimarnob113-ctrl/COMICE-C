# IRONBLOOD: FIRST BLOOD (Origin Manga Script)
**Setting:** Old Dhaka Core (Monochrome Ash) → The Sky-Scrap (Orange Dieselpunk)
**Style:** Metal Slug Arcade Pixel Art / Seinen Action Manga

---

## SCENE 1: THE ASH-SEALED CITY (Old Dhaka Core)

### PAGE 1
**PANEL 1 (Large Splash - Pure Monochrome Black & White):**
The sealed ruins of Old Dhaka. Massive blast doors cracked open. The entire cityscape is drowned in pale grey ash and heavy fog. Complete dead silence. 
Drifting in the fog are glowing **stark red optical sensors** of spider-like AXIOM defense drones.

**CAPTION:**
*Old Dhaka. Sealed for fifteen years.*
*The air smells like rusted iron and burned memory.*

**PANEL 2:**
A 16-year-old **JAMAL** in worn sneakers tries jimmying open a heavy server bunker door with a rusted crowbar. He slips on the ash.

**PANEL 3:**
A heavy combat boot lands next to Jamal's head. **KAEL** stands over him, wearing clean soldier fatigue armor, his giant cleaver *The Shard* unblemished by green veins (this is before the Siphon).

**KAEL:**
You’re loud enough to wake the dead, kid.

**JAMAL (scrambling up):**
Hey! I have a contract for this server! The broker hired ME!

**KAEL (pulling out an identical encrypted cipher chip):**
The broker hired both of us. Step aside.

---

### PAGE 2
**PANEL 1:**
Inside the bunker. **USTAD** is already tied into the main terminal harness, sweat dripping into his mustache, tool belt overflowing.

**USTAD:**
Stop yelling! AXIOM's military encryption has 128 layers. One wrong pulse and the automated defense grid turns this whole block into soup!

**PANEL 2:**
Red alarm strobes suddenly flare through the monochrome fog outside. Hundreds of red drone eyes light up in the mist.

**SFX:**
*WUUUUUURN - WUUUUUURN!*

**PANEL 3:**
A heavy drone fires an armor-piercing projectile straight at Kael. 

**PANEL 4 (Speedline Splash):**
A metallic whip-sword flashes through the air in a graceful ribbon loop, slicing the projectile in two and cutting three drones in half.
**SUNEHARA** lands in an elegant dancer pose, her magenta-and-pink hair flowing around her ornate crimson combat sari.

**SUNEHARA (smiling warmly at Kael):**
Hello, boys! Don’t worry, you’re doing great! Keep hacking!

---

### PAGE 3
**PANEL 1:**
A massive quad-legged AXIOM enforcer mech bursts through the concrete ceiling, aiming twin rotary miniguns at Sunehara and Jamal.

**PANEL 2:**
A compact, poised woman in a spotless double-breasted white lab coat over a high-collared dark purple tunic walks calmly out of the smoke. **ZINNIA**, her long dark hair pinned in a sleek braided updo with a silver needle pin, adjusts her round gold wireframe glasses with an unblinking, pleasant smile.

**ZINNIA:**
My, what an aggressive chassis. You really should take something for that blood pressure.

**PANEL 3:**
Zinnia tosses a fragile glass vial right into the mech's intake valve. 
A violent purple chemical reaction sizzles. The metal dissolves into frothing slag in three seconds. The pilot collapses, paralyzed.

**SFX:**
*FZZZZZZZZT!*

**PANEL 4:**
A second wave of fifty drones approaches from the elevated highway 800 meters away.

**PANEL 5 (High Angle):**
On top of a ruined clocktower in the fog, **KPOP NINJA** crouches behind a massive matte-black anti-materiel sniper rifle. He is wearing a tight black short-sleeved compression shirt, baggy off-white martial arts pants, with silver-white spiky hair and wide-open, radiant sapphire-blue eyes glowing through the ash. He has a predator's grin.

**KPOP NINJA (whispering into his comms):**
Chat, witness perfection.

**SFX:**
*BOOOOOOOM! BOOOOOM! BOOOOOM!*

**PANEL 6:**
Three clean tracer rounds ignite across the monochrome sky, detonating the lead drones in chain-reaction fireballs.

---

## SCENE 2: THE ASCENT TO THE SKY-SCRAP

### PAGE 4
**PANEL 1 (Color Explosion - Hazy Orange & Diesel Smoke):**
The aesthetic dramatically shifts. The crew breaches through an upper air shaft into **THE SKY-SCRAP**. 
Miles above the ash, a roaring, vertical shantytown made of lashed-together zeppelins, steel cables, and spinning propellers hangs in the orange smog.

**USTAD (holding the extracted drive):**
I got the manifest! But AXIOM just deployed an orbital hunter gunship! We have to reach the cargo zeppelin at the peak!

**PANEL 2:**
An enormous AXIOM Hunter Gunship descends from the clouds, searchlights blinding the swaying wooden platforms.

**PANEL 3:**
Kael steps to the front, cleaver drawn. 
Beside him: Jamal holding his short blades, Sunehara coiling her whip-sword, Zinnia loading fresh toxic injectors, KPop Ninja racking a heavy sniper bolt, and Ustad rigging the zipline winch.

**KAEL:**
Move together or die alone. Move!

---

### PAGE 5 (Climax & Escape)
**PANEL 1 (Double Spread):**
The crew fights in unison across the swaying sky platforms. 
KPop Ninja takes out the gunship's thrusters; Sunehara cleans the boarding troops off the railings; Zinnia melts the main engine hull; Kael delivers a colossal cleaver chop to the control cockpit.

**PANEL 2:**
Jamal catches the trailing cable of a departing cargo zeppelin, securing it so the six of them can scramble aboard.

**PANEL 3:**
The gunship plummets into the orange clouds below, exploding against the ruined skyscraper peaks.

---

## SCENE 3: THE EPILOGUE & THE MASTERMIND

### PAGE 6
**PANEL 1:**
Inside the quiet cargo hold of the zeppelin at sunset. 
Ustad plugs the manifest drive into a screen. A detailed map of **Noakhali District** projects into the air, labeled: *PROJECT SIPHON - PHASE 1 OCCUPATION*.

**JAMAL (shocked):**
Noakhali... that’s my neighborhood. They're going to take everything.

**KAEL:**
Not if we're waiting for them.

**PANEL 2:**
The main radio console crackles to life. An encrypted frequency connects with crystal-clear audio.

**RADIO VOICE:**
*Impressive. Six strangers who couldn’t stand each other... and you brought down a Hunter gunship.*

**PANEL 3:**
Kael steps up to the radio.

**KAEL:**
Who is this? The broker was supposed to meet us at the drop point.

**PANEL 4 (Shadowed Silhouette):**
A flash of a man in a perfectly tailored coat, holding a carved cane, watching the zeppelin from a penthouse in the distant Dubai Quarter. **JAHANGIR BHAI**.

**JAHANGIR BHAI (over comms):**
*The broker was an audition, son. AXIOM is bringing an army to Noakhali in three years. I needed to see who had the iron in their blood to fight them.*
*Rest up. When you get to the block... come find me. I’ll be the only clean man in the room.*

**PANEL 5 (Final Shot):**
Kael, Jamal, Ustad, Sunehara, Zinnia, and KPop Ninja standing on the zeppelin deck, looking out toward the horizon where the dark storm clouds over Noakhali gather.

**CAPTION:**
*Three years later, AXIOM arrived in Noakhali.*
*The resistance was already born.*
