# IRONBLOOD: SURAIYA — The Gilded Lotus

**True Name:** Suraiya  
**Hero Codename:** **GILDED LOTUS** (Public Title: *The Diva*)  
**Archetype:** Flamboyant Combat Diva / Emotional Heart of the Crew (Mitsuri Kanroji energy)  
**Status:** Core Resistance Vanguard & Cultural Icon  

---

## 👑 1. CHARACTER CONCEPT & VIBE

Suraiya is a radiant, larger-than-life warrior-diva. Before the occupation, she was the nation's biggest superstar performer. When AXIOM banned independent culture, she brought her theatrical flair, boundless energy, and deadly segmented ribbon whip-sword straight to the front lines.

- **The Personality:** Aggressively kind, energetic, emotional, and fitness-obsessed. She loves high-protein sweet confections (*saffron-pistachio protein sandesh*) and refuses to let anyone go into combat under-fueled.
- **Combat Style:** Acrobatic dance-combat with a segmented razor-sharp ribbon blade that coils and lashes across wide areas, slicing through drone swarms with theatrical elegance.

---

## 💬 2. KEY RELATIONSHIPS & DYNAMICS

### With KAEL ("Kael-Bhaiyaaa!"):
Suraiya is the younger sister figure Kael never asked for but fiercely protects. 
- While everyone else walks on eggshells around Kael's brooding trauma, Suraiya treats him with shameless affection:
  > *"Kael-bhaiyaaa! Look at you, you're bleeding again! Sit down and eat this protein sandesh right now or I will tie you down with my ribbon!"*
- Kael grumbles, acts annoyed, but always eats the sandesh. She is one of the few anchors keeping his humanity intact.

### With GOVERNOR MUJIB ("The Muzan Terror"):
Unlike the street thugs or corporate mercs, Governor Mujib genuinely terrifies Suraiya.
- To her, Governor Mujib is not just a politician; he is like **Muzan Kibutsuji**—an ancient, towering sovereign entity whose sheer gravitational presence makes her breath catch in her throat.
  > *"Kael-bhai... that man isn't human. When he looks at you through those black frames... it feels like the air itself is bowing down. Do not go near him alone."*

### With ZINNIA:
They are total opposites who adore each other. Suraiya provides the loud, theatrical cheer; Zinnia provides the quiet, smiling clinical menace. Suraiya constantly compliments Zinnia's braided hair while Zinnia custom-synthesizes non-stick polish for Suraiya's ribbon-sword.
