## 📖 ACT-BY-ACT MISSION & CUTSCENE BREAKDOWN

### 🌧️ ACT I: THE FLOODED BLOCK (Noakhali Uprising)
*Setting:* The waterlogged stilt-slums of Noakhali District. Pouring rain, neon graffiti, AXIOM checkpoints.

* **Mission 1: The Night Checkpoint**
  * Kael and his modified combat mech **Borkot** breach an AXIOM pacification perimeter. High-impact beat-em-up street brawling.
* **Mission 2: Ustad's Workshop**
  * Meeting **Ustad** in his dry-dock garage. Ustad repairs Borkot's armor while young **Jamal** feeds intel on AXIOM supply routes.
* **Mission 3: The Labor Compound**
  * Liberating hundreds of displaced workers forced into Siphon extraction labor.
* **Boss Fight: THE WARDEN**
  * Heavy Iron Collar enforcer with a shock whip. Kael cleaves through his armor, unlocking his first Siphon Rage surge.

---

### 🚤 ACT II: THE WET BAZAARS & THE ANCIENT SOLDIER
*Setting:* The sprawling floating black markets on the flooded canals & the submerged vaults of Old Dhaka.

* **Mission 4: The Godfather's Barge**
  * Kael travels through the neon-lit *Wet Bazaars* to meet **Jahangir Bhai** aboard his luxury mahogany flagship. 
  * Jahangir agrees to supply military-grade ordnance and access codes if Kael sabotages AXIOM's automated tax nodes.
* **Mission 5: Sublevel 7 — The Cryo-Vault**
  * Infiltrating the sealed Old Dhaka Grid Authority building to steal an energy capacitor.
  * **The Awakening of BAHADUR:** Kael unseals a Mark-IV cryo-pod, thawing the 2029 Inqilab Force super-soldier after 41 years on ice. Bahadur cracks off the frost, grabs his bronze shield, and helps the crew smash an AXIOM ambush!
* **Boss Fight: COMMANDANT VOSS**
  * AXIOM cyber-exoskeleton commander attempting to purge the vault. Bahadur deflects Voss’s railgun with his shield while Kael lands the finishing strike.

---

### 🌿 ACT III: THE BIOLUME MANGROVES & THE SAGE
*Setting:* Glowing teal bioluminescent mangrove canals & The Archive of the Drowned Dawn.

* **Mission 6: The Glass-Bark Swamps**
  * Navigating radar-jamming glass mangrove roots. **Suraiya (Gilded Lotus)** clears drone swarms with her ribbon whip-sword while **Zinnia** uses synthetic enzymes to dissolve toxic barriers.
* **Mission 7: The Gunslinger's Flank**
  * While Kael advances, a massive AXIOM armored column attempts to flank them. 
  * **Surjo** (*The Siphon Plant*) and **KPop Ninja** (*Arman*) stage an insane long-range sniper/ricochet tag-team defense, obliterating the column without harming the surrounding ecosystem.
* **Mission 8: The Drowned Archive**
  * Kael enters the dry, warm-lit sunken library beneath the waters and meets **MASTER DIGANTA**.
  * Diganta casts spinning golden Dr. Strange-style mandalas to dissolve Kael's volatile energy rage, teaches him that the Siphon is Earth's living heart, and bestows the acoustic frequency key to the core.

---

### 🏛️ ACT IV: THE THRONE OF TIDES
*Setting:* The elevated, rain-swept luxury skyline of the Dubai Quarter & The Floating Presidential Pavilion (*The Bongo Dreadnought*).

* **Mission 9: The Sky-Spire Ascent**
  * Scaling the corporate skyscrapers. Ustad rigs gravity ziplines, Arman provides sniper overwatch, and Suraiya infiltrates the security ballroom in her crimson-gold combat sari.
* **Mission 10: The Sovereign's Chamber**
  * Confronting **Governor Mujib** in his glass-domed grand pavilion.
* **Boss Fight: GOVERNOR MUJIB (*The Sovereign Titan*)**
  * *Phase 1 (State Decree):* Smoking his amber pipe while commanding orbital laser grids and Red Directorate shield guards.
  * *Phase 2 (Iron Will):* Sub-dermal titanium rig activation—explosive kinetic martial arts and heavy shockwaves.
  * *Phase 3 (Sovereign Pressure):* Gravitational crushing aura (Darth Vader tier) and crimson authority fissures.
* **The Outcome:** Mujib is defeated but **survives**. He respects Kael's unyielding fire, warns him of AXIOM's central armada, and withdraws to his bunker as a neutral shadow titan.

---

### 🧬 ACT V: THE LIVING CORE (The Descent & The Finale)
*Setting:* The deep subterranean mantle 5 miles beneath the delta. Pulsing organic walls, ancient bio-magnetic rivers.

* **Mission 11: The Tectonic Chasm**
  * The united crew (Kael, Borkot, Bahadur, Suraiya, Zinnia, Arman, Surjo, and Ustad) breaches the deep mantle, battling through waves of corrupted Siphon bio-drones.
* **Final Boss: THE SIPHON HERALD**
  * A colossal, shifting eldritch titan created when corporate toxic drill waste fused with the wounded planetary core.
  * The crew fights in full synergy: Arman snipes weak points; Suraiya clears tendrils; Zinnia injects anti-corrosive enzymes; Bahadur tanks the tectonic slams with his bronze shield; Surjo opens the core with bio-kinetic wings; Kael delivers the final cleave.

---

## 🌅 THE CANON FINALE: THE SOVEREIGN GUARDIAN (Ending B)

### The Climax & Ascension:
* Standing over the defeated Siphon Herald in the collapsing core, Kael looks at the massive extraction drills and remembers everything AXIOM took: his wife, his 5-year-old daughter, his commander, and decades of stolen lives.
* He rejects a gentle compromise. Kael plunges both bare hands directly into the raw, unbridled Siphon planetary mantle.
* **The Transfiguration:** The Siphon’s primordial bio-magnetic energy surges through his bloodstream. His tactical armor shatters as veins of blinding celestial teal and dark obsidian armor erupt across his body. *The Shard* fuses permanently with his right arm, becoming an extension of the earth's living tectonic wrath.
* **The Impenetrable Sovereign Veil:** A colossal bio-magnetic pulse erupts outward from the delta. Across the entire nation of Bangladesh:
  - Every AXIOM orbital satellite, drone lane, and corporate surveillance grid is permanently fried.
  - An impenetrable storm veil of bio-magnetic auroras and raging tempest winds seals the borders of the country from the outside world forever.
  - Foreign empires, corporate armadas, and colonial fleets can never set foot on delta soil again.

### The Epilogue:
On a high stone outcrop overlooking the flooded, neon-lit skyline of Noakhali at midnight, a towering silhouette stands silent in the storm. His eyes burn with eternal, unblinking teal fire. 

Governor Mujib watches from his floating pavilion, raising a glass in grim respect. Jahangir Bhai leans on his mahogany barge railing, exhaling cigar smoke into the rain with a knowing grin. 

**Kael has ceased to be just a soldier.**  
**He has become the immortal, eternal Sovereign Guardian of the Iron Delta.**
