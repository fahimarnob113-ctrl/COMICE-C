# IRONBLOOD: New Location Concepts (Aesthetic Expansion)

If you want to break away from the wet, neon-drenched cyberpunk streets of Noakhali Block and the sterile blue corporate look of the Dubai Quarter, here are four completely different environments that fit into the IRONBLOOD world. 

These are perfect for DLC, later acts, or spin-off comic pages. Each comes with a ready-to-copy image generation prompt.

---

## 1. THE SUNKEN SUNDARBANS (Bio-Punk / Overgrown Jungle)
**The Concept:** Nature aggressively reclaiming abandoned tech. South of the city, the great mangrove forests absorbed a massive Siphon energy leak. Now, the jungle is overgrown, mutated, and suffocatingly vibrant. Rusted AXIOM mechs are trapped in giant glowing roots. 
**Visual Palette:** Suffocating greens, bright golds, and pulsing organic bioluminescence. Extreme sunlight filtering through thick canopy.
**Vibe:** *Horizon Zero Dawn* meets the classic jungle levels of *Metal Slug 3*.

> **Copy/Paste Prompt:**
> `Background concept art, THE SUNKEN SUNDARBANS. A dense, overgrown mutated mangrove jungle reclaiming a ruined cyberpunk city. Giant twisting tree roots are crushing rusted, abandoned mecha tanks. Vibrant, suffocating green and gold foliage with pulsing bioluminescent flora. Heavy sunlight filtering through a thick canopy. 16-bit pixel art, Metal Slug jungle aesthetic, highly detailed crunchy textures, no anti-aliasing.`

---

## 2. THE SKY-SCRAP (Diesel-Punk / Aerial Slum)
**The Concept:** For those trying to escape the floods and the Siphon entirely. A sprawling, vertical shantytown suspended high in the air, lashed together with heavy cables between the peaks of old skyscrapers and massive, rusted zeppelins. Constant howling winds and spinning propellers.
**Visual Palette:** Hazy, smoggy orange skies, endless brown rusted metal, frayed ropes, and sputtering black diesel smoke.
**Vibe:** *Mad Max: Fury Road* suspended thousands of feet in the air. 

> **Copy/Paste Prompt:**
> `Background concept art, THE SKY-SCRAP. A sprawling, chaotic diesel-punk shantytown suspended high in the sky between ruined skyscraper peaks. Lashed together with heavy cables, scrap metal, and massive rusted zeppelins. Spinning airplane propellers and black exhaust smoke. Hazy, smog-filled orange sky. 16-bit pixel art, Metal Slug aesthetic, gritty industrial sky-city, highly detailed crunchy textures.`

---

## 3. THE GLASS WASTES (Crystal Apocalypse)
**The Concept:** The site of the first major Siphon energy surge. The heat was so intense it instantly flash-boiled the floodwaters and fused the sand and concrete into massive, jagged dunes of teal glass. It is completely barren, silent, and deadly sharp.
**Visual Palette:** Blinding white daylight, shattered teal and cyan glass, sharp geometric reflections. Almost zero shadow. 
**Vibe:** A stark, beautiful, but utterly dead crystalline desert.

> **Copy/Paste Prompt:**
> `Background concept art, THE GLASS WASTES. A post-apocalyptic desert where the ground and ruined city buildings have been fused into massive, jagged dunes of sharp teal glass. Blindingly bright white daylight reflecting off crystalline surfaces. Completely barren and silent. 16-bit pixel art, sharp geometric crystal aesthetic, high contrast, vibrant teal and cyan.`

---

## 4. THE OLD DHAKA CORE (Monochrome Ghost Town)
**The Concept:** A sector of the old capital that was sealed behind massive blast doors before the floods. The atmosphere inside is entirely choked with thick, pale ash. Color doesn't seem to exist here. The only light comes from the glowing red optics of automated, centuries-old defense drones still patrolling the silent streets.
**Visual Palette:** Strict monochrome (black, white, thick grey fog) with stark, piercing red accents (enemy eyes, laser sights).
**Vibe:** *Silent Hill* meets *Limbo*. Oppressive, quiet, and deeply unsettling.

> **Copy/Paste Prompt:**
> `Background concept art, THE OLD DHAKA CORE. A completely silent, abandoned cyberpunk city street choked in thick, pale grey ash and heavy fog. The entire scene is strictly monochrome black, white, and grey. The only color is the stark, glowing neon red optics of a skeletal defense drone in the fog. 16-bit pixel art, oppressive atmospheric ghost town, noir cyberpunk, highly detailed.`
