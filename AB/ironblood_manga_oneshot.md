# IRONBLOOD: THE AWAKENING (One-Shot Manga Script)
**Pages:** ~35 pages
**Style:** Seinen action (Dark, gritty, high contrast inks, heavy speedlines during combat)
**Read Direction:** Right-to-Left (Traditional Manga)

---

## PAGE 1 (Full Page Splash)
**PANEL 1 (Full Page):** 
A sprawling, towering view of **Noakhali Block, 2070**. It’s raining heavily. The architecture is a chaotic stack of rusted corrugated metal, solar mesh, and concrete built on stilts above dark, flooded streets. High above, pristine corporate drones fly toward the gleaming towers of the **Dubai Quarter** in the distance. The contrast between the filthy, neon-lit slums and the corporate paradise is stark. 

**CAPTIONBOX (CIPHER):** 
They promised us the future. 

**CAPTIONBOX (CIPHER):** 
AXIOM Corporation. They brought the grid. They brought the jobs.

**CAPTIONBOX (CIPHER):** 
Then they brought the walls. 

**TITLE LOGO (Bottom Right):** 
**IRONBLOOD**

---

## PAGE 2
**PANEL 1:** 
Street level. A narrow, flooded alleyway lit by the harsh pink and cyan glow of a broken noodle stand sign. Three **Iron Collar Heavies** (AXIOM's hired thugs in rusted armor) are cornering a young kid. It’s **JAMAL**, wearing his oversized jacket and a backwards cap. 

**IRON COLLAR 1:** 
You’re out past curfew, rat. 

**JAMAL (smirking, defiant):** 
Curfew is for people with homes, big guy. I’m just taking a walk.

**PANEL 2:** 
The Heavy grabs Jamal by the collar, lifting him off the ground effortlessly. Jamal’s smirk falters slightly. 

**IRON COLLAR 1:** 
Funny. Let's see if you can walk with broken knees. 

**PANEL 3:** 
A shadow falls over the alley entrance. The Heavy stops, looking up. 

**PANEL 4:** 
Silhouette shot. A broad, muscular figure stands at the mouth of the alley. Water drips off a massive, jagged slab of iron strapped to his back. It’s **KAEL**. 

**KAEL:** 
Put him down.

---

## PAGE 3
**PANEL 1:** 
Close up on the Iron Collar Heavy, sneering. 

**IRON COLLAR 1:** 
Who the hell are— 

**PANEL 2 (Large Panel):** 
KAEL steps into the neon light. His eyes are glowing a violent, unnatural **teal-green**. Vein-like energy scars pulse up his left arm. He looks exhausted, furious, and terrifying. 

**KAEL:** 
I said. Put him down. 

**PANEL 3:** 
The Heavy drops Jamal and pulls a heavy stun baton. The baton crackles with blue electricity. The other two thugs draw their weapons. 

**IRON COLLAR 1:** 
Kill him.

---

## PAGE 4 & 5 (Double Page Spread)
**PANEL 1 (Spanning both pages):** 
Absolute kinetic violence. KAEL hasn't even drawn his sword. He steps inside the first Heavy’s guard, his fist buried deep into the armored chest plate of the thug. The armor shatters inward. Shockwaves ripple through the rain. Heavy speedlines. 

**SFX (Massive text):** 
**KRAAAAASH!**

**PANEL 2:** 
The second thug swings a machete. Kael ducks effortlessly, catching the thug's wrist in his glowing left hand. 

**SFX:** 
*CRACK*

**PANEL 3:** 
Kael throws the thug into the brick wall so hard the concrete splinters. 

**PANEL 4:** 
The third thug, trembling, drops his weapon and runs for his life into the rain. Kael doesn't chase him. He just breathes out, breath visible in the cold air. His green eyes slowly dim back to a normal dark brown. 

---

## PAGE 6
**PANEL 1:** 
Jamal picks himself up out of the puddles, brushing off his jacket. He looks at Kael with wide eyes. 

**JAMAL:** 
Bhai... they said you left the district. 

**PANEL 2:** 
Kael doesn't look at him. He stares at his own left hand. The green veins fade, but a faint, rhythmic pulse remains under his skin. 

**KAEL:** 
I tried. I didn't get far. 

**PANEL 3:** 
Suddenly, a massive spotlight clicks on, blinding them. The light pours down from an elevated walkway above the alley. 

**SFX:** 
*ZZZZZZAK!* 

**PANEL 4:** 
Looking up from Kael’s perspective. Standing on the walkway is **THE WARDEN**. Massive, clad in heavy riveted armor, his half-mask gleaming in the rain. He holds a thick chain whip. 

**THE WARDEN:** 
Kael. You deserted your post. And now you’re assaulting AXIOM personnel. 

---

## PAGE 7
**PANEL 1:** 
Kael reaches over his shoulder, gripping the hilt of his massive cleaver, *The Shard*. 

**KAEL:** 
They aren't personnel, Warden. They're thugs. Just like you. 

**PANEL 2:** 
The Warden drops from the walkway, landing in the alley with a bone-rattling thud. The water splashes violently around his heavy boots. 

**SFX:** 
*DOOOM*

**THE WARDEN:** 
We are order. You are a dying breed of chaos. 

**PANEL 3:** 
The Warden whips his chain forward. It blurs with speed. 

**SFX:** 
*WHSSSH!*

---

## PAGE 8
**PANEL 1:** 
Kael draws *The Shard* just in time. The heavy chain wraps around the massive blade, sparks flying as iron grinds against iron. 

**SFX:** 
*SKREEEEEECH!* 

**PANEL 2:** 
The Warden yanks the chain hard, trying to disarm Kael. Kael is dragged forward a few feet, boots sliding on the wet pavement. 

**PANEL 3:** 
Close up on Kael’s face. He’s struggling. The Warden is physically stronger. 
But then... the veins on Kael’s neck begin to glow green again. 

**KAEL (internal monologue):** 
*Not again. Don't let it out.*

**PANEL 4:** 
A voice speaks in Kael’s mind. A deep, ancient rumble, presented in inverted text bubbles (black bubble, white text). 

**THE SIPHON (VOICE):** 
**LET US OUT.**

---

## PAGE 9
**PANEL 1 (Large Panel):** 
Kael enters **RAGE MODE**. An explosive aura of teal-green energy erupts from his body, vaporizing the rain around him. His eyes go completely white with power. 

**SFX:** 
*VWOOOOOOOM!*

**PANEL 2:** 
The Warden looks genuinely shocked behind his mask. 

**THE WARDEN:** 
What are you...? 

**PANEL 3:** 
Kael pulls back on the sword. With his augmented strength, he yanks the Warden forward off his feet. 

**PANEL 4:** 
Kael swings *The Shard* in a massive, sweeping arc. The impact throws The Warden straight through the rusted metal wall of a nearby building. 

**SFX:** 
*BA-BAAAAM!*

---

## PAGE 10
**PANEL 1:** 
Silence in the alley. The Warden is buried under rubble, unconscious. 
Kael stands there, panting heavily. The green energy slowly recedes, leaving him looking frail and exhausted. He drops to one knee, using the sword as a crutch. 

**JAMAL (off-panel):** 
Bhai... what was that? 

**PANEL 2:** 
A new voice echoes from the darkness. Mechanical, deep, and steady. 

**BORKOT (off-panel):** 
It is a symptom. Of proximity. 

**PANEL 3:** 
**BORKOT** steps into the light. The massive, reprogrammed AXIOM robot. Matte black chassis, glowing blue optics. He towers over Kael. 

**BORKOT:** 
You came back. That is within acceptable parameters of loyalty. 

---

## PAGE 11
**PANEL 1:** 
Kael looks up at Borkot, a tired smirk crossing his face. 

**KAEL:** 
You’re looking banged up, tin-man. 

**PANEL 2:** 
Jamal pulls a small, blinking data-drive from his pocket and holds it out. 

**JAMAL:** 
I didn't just come out here to get beat up, Kael. Ustad fixed the receiver. We got a transmission. Encrypted. 
It’s from inside AXIOM. 

**PANEL 3:** 
Close up on the blinking drive in Jamal's hand. 

**JAMAL:** 
Someone named "Cipher." They said you need to see this. 

---

## PAGE 12
**PANEL 1:** 
Cut to a small, hidden safehouse (Ustad's workshop). Cables everywhere, old CRT monitors glowing. **USTAD**, a stocky man in a dirty shirt and yellow hard-hat, plugs the drive into a terminal. 

**USTAD:** 
I had to bypass three firewalls for this. If this blows my rig, you're buying me a new one, Kael. 

**PANEL 2:** 
A voice waveform appears on the screen. **CIPHER’S** voice plays. 

**CIPHER (Terminal):** 
*Kael. If you are hearing this, it means you survived the perimeter.*

**PANEL 3:** 
The screen flashes, displaying a wireframe blueprint of the district. Deep underground, a massive, pulsing anomaly is highlighted in red. 

**CIPHER (Terminal):** 
*AXIOM didn’t come to Noakhali to build infrastructure. They came because they found something buried underneath it. They call it The Siphon.*

---

## PAGE 13
**PANEL 1:** 
Close up on Kael, staring at the screen. His eyes reflect the red glow of the anomaly. 

**CIPHER (Terminal):** 
*It’s ancient. It’s powerful. And their machines are waking it up. The power you feel in your blood, Kael? It’s bleeding into you. It's bleeding into the whole city.* 

**PANEL 2:** 
Borkot looks at Kael. 

**BORKOT:** 
If they fully awaken it, AXIOM will not be able to control it. The casualty projection for this district is one hundred percent. 

**PANEL 3:** 
Kael grips the hilt of his sword tightly. 

**KAEL:** 
Where is the core?

**CIPHER (Terminal):** 
*Directly beneath the Dubai Quarter. Commandant Voss is guarding the descent elevator.* 

---

## PAGE 14 (Final Page)
**PANEL 1:** 
Kael turns toward the exit of the workshop. He throws a heavy trench coat over his armor. 

**KAEL:** 
Ustad, keep the comms open. Jamal, stay off the streets. 

**PANEL 2:** 
Borkot steps up beside Kael, his blue optics shining in the dark. 

**BORKOT:** 
I am coming with you. 

**PANEL 3 (Large Bottom Panel):** 
Exterior shot. Kael and Borkot stand on a rusted rooftop, looking out across the flooded, neon-drenched city. In the far distance, the pristine corporate towers of the Dubai Quarter pierce the sky, surrounded by searchlights. 

**KAEL:** 
They took our streets. Now they want to take what's underneath them. 

**KAEL (Smirking):** 
Let's go to work.

---

# PART 2: THE FACILITY (Dubai Quarter)

## PAGE 15
**PANEL 1 (Large Horizontal):**
Establishing shot. The **Dubai Quarter**. The stark contrast to Noakhali Block is jarring. Here, the rain reflects off pristine, high-tech black asphalt. Massive brutalist corporate towers branded with the glowing blue **AXIOM** logo dominate the skyline. In the foreground, heavy, rusted AXIOM armored personnel carriers (classic Metal Slug chunky tank design) idle at a checkpoint.

**CAPTIONBOX (KAEL):**
The Dubai Quarter. They call it the forward base. 
I call it the front door to hell.

**PANEL 2:**
Close up on an AXIOM Grunt's boot splashing into a puddle. He is holding a sleek, heavy energy rifle.

**PANEL 3:**
Behind the Grunt, in the deep shadows of an exhaust vent, a massive iron cleaver quietly rises.

**PANEL 4:**
Kael steps out of the shadow, his eyes glowing a faint teal. He brings the flat of *The Shard* down on the back of the Grunt's helmet.

**SFX:**
*THWACK!*

---

## PAGE 16
**PANEL 1:**
The checkpoint alarms blare. Red strobe lights wash over the scene. A squad of six AXIOM Grunts open fire. Muzzle flashes light up the rain.

**SFX:**
*BR-BR-BR-BR-BR!*

**PANEL 2:**
Kael isn't there. He’s already moving, a blur of motion. He slides under the barrage of heavy caliber fire, his sword trailing sparks against the asphalt.

**PANEL 3:**
Suddenly, the massive AXIOM armored carrier shudders violently. 
A gigantic, matte-black robotic hand punches straight through the side plating of the vehicle. 

**SFX:**
*KRAK-THOOM!*

**PANEL 4:**
**BORKOT** rips the side door clean off its hinges, tossing it into the squad of Grunts like a frisbee. His blue optics flare in the red alarm light.

**BORKOT:**
Perimeter breached. Advancing to extraction feed towers.

---

## PAGE 17
**PANEL 1:**
Kael and Borkot stand back-to-back in the middle of the street. Dozens of AXIOM soldiers are pouring out of the surrounding buildings. Above them, automated turret drones begin to lock on.

**KAEL:**
There’s too many, Borkot. We need to break the line.

**PANEL 2:**
A sudden, deafening mechanical roar tears through the air from the rooftops above. 

**SFX:**
*WRRRRRRRRRR-CHK-CHK-CHK-CHK-CHK!*

**PANEL 3 (Large Panel):**
Heavy, explosive tracer rounds completely obliterate the AXIOM turret drones in the sky. Concrete shatters as the high-caliber rounds chew through the corporate barricades, tearing the street apart in a straight line.

**PANEL 4:**
Kael looks up toward the source of the fire. 

---

## PAGE 18
**PANEL 1:**
Standing on the edge of a rain-slicked corporate billboard is **MASUD**. He wears heavy, salvaged tactical armor. A massive, terrifying Gatling gun is bolted directly to his right shoulder mount, smoking heavily from the barrels. 

**MASUD (smirking behind his tactical visor):**
I don’t work for free, Kael! 

**PANEL 2:**
Masud leaps from the billboard, landing on the roof of a destroyed AXIOM carrier. The suspension completely collapses under his immense weight.

**SFX:**
*KRUUUNCH!*

**MASUD:**
But for a chance to blow up AXIOM's feed towers? I’ll make an exception! Keep them busy! I’ll crack the armor!

**PANEL 3:**
Kael grips his sword with both hands. The teal-green veins on his arm flare brightly. He grins.

**KAEL:**
You heard the man, Borkot. 

**PANEL 4:**
Kael charges directly into the remaining AXIOM squad, his sword drawn back for a devastating horizontal slash.

**KAEL:**
Let's crack some armor!

---

# PART 3: THE DESCENT (The Seattle Depths)

## PAGE 19
**PANEL 1 (Large Panel):**
The Dubai Quarter is burning. The massive AXIOM feed towers are collapsing in a shower of sparks and black smoke, thanks to Masud’s heavy artillery. 

**PANEL 2:**
Kael stands over the defeated, sparking exoskeleton of **COMMANDANT VOSS**. Voss is badly beaten, his high-tech visor cracked in half, revealing a terrified human eye beneath.

**COMMANDANT VOSS (coughing blood):**
You think... you saved them? The feed towers were stabilizing it. 
You just let it off the leash.

**PANEL 3:**
The ground violently shakes. 
A massive, jagged fissure splits the asphalt of the Dubai Quarter. Blinding teal light erupts from the crack, shooting straight up into the rain.

**SFX:**
*KRAK-KOOOOOOM!*

---

## PAGE 20
**PANEL 1:**
Kael and Borkot peer over the edge of the massive fissure. It goes down for miles.

**CIPHER (via Kael’s earpiece):**
*Kael. The core is exposed. It’s pulling energy from the whole district. If it fully wakes, there won't be a city left to save.*

**PANEL 2:**
Kael grips *The Shard*. The teal veins on his arm are glowing brighter than ever. He looks at Borkot.

**KAEL:**
Going down.

**PANEL 3 (Vertical Panel):**
Kael and Borkot plummet down the chasm, sliding along a massive, rusted industrial pipe as they descend into the darkness. 

---

## PAGE 21
**PANEL 1 (Half Page Splash):**
**THE SEATTLE DEPTHS**. They land in a massive, cavernous underground cavern. It is literally raining inside the cave, water pouring from broken city pipes above. Everything is rusted iron, completely overgrown with glowing, bioluminescent teal moss and fungi. 

**PANEL 2:**
Hollow Vein creatures—twisted, corrupted humans with glowing teal eyes—skitter along the walls in the darkness, watching them, but they don't attack. 

**BORKOT:**
They are not engaging. 

**PANEL 3:**
Kael looks at his arm. The glowing veins are pulsing in exact rhythm with the ambient light of the cavern.

**KAEL:**
They think I’m one of them. 

---

## PAGE 22
**PANEL 1:**
They reach the entrance to **THE DEEP WILD**. The aesthetic shifts violently. Massive, jagged black stone slabs and ancient, rotted red wood Torii gates stand in the subterranean dark, choked by thick, pulsing teal roots.

**PANEL 2:**
Borkot takes a step forward. Suddenly, his massive mechanical chassis sparks violently. The teal roots recoil, lashing out like whips to strike his armor.

**SFX:**
*KZZZZZT!*

**PANEL 3:**
Borkot drops to one knee, his systems failing. 

**BORKOT:**
My chassis. The environment... it rejects synthetic matter. I cannot proceed.

**PANEL 4:**
Kael steps past the Torii gate. The roots part for him. He looks back at his massive friend.

**KAEL:**
Hold the line, Borkot. 

**BORKOT:**
Return, Kael. That is an order.

---

# PART 4: THE CORE (The Deep Wild)

## PAGE 23
**PANEL 1 (Large Horizontal):**
Kael walks alone into the massive central chamber. The air is thick with teal fog. At the center is a massive, beating heart made of black organic matter and crystal.

**PANEL 2:**
The Siphon matter shifts. It pulls together, bones cracking, organic sludge forming a shape.

**PANEL 3:**
**THE SIPHON HERALD** stands before Kael. An elongated, terrifying four-armed humanoid. It has no face, just a hollow void with two piercing teal eyes.

**THE SIPHON HERALD (Inverted text bubble):**
**YOU ARE ALREADY INSIDE.**

---

## PAGE 24 & 25 (Double Page Spread)
**PANEL 1 (Full Spread):**
The ultimate clash. The Herald swings a massive, corrupted battle-axe. Kael meets it with *The Shard*. The impact creates a shockwave that blows the rain and fog away in a massive circle around them. 

**SFX (Massive):**
*DOOOOOOOOOM!*

**PANEL 2 (Inset):**
The Herald’s two secondary arms lash out, slashing Kael across the chest. Blood hits the stone.

**PANEL 3 (Inset):**
Kael stumbles back. He is outmatched. The Herald raises its axe for the killing blow.

**THE SIPHON HERALD:**
**WE REMEMBER YOU. WE ARE YOU.**

---

## PAGE 26
**PANEL 1:**
Close up on Kael’s face. He closes his eyes. He stops fighting the corruption.

**KAEL (Internal):**
*Then use it.*

**PANEL 2:**
Kael’s eyes snap open. They are pure, blinding, luminous teal. The veins on his arm spread across his chest, up his neck, glowing with the exact same intensity as the Herald. 

**PANEL 3:**
Kael moves faster than light. He steps inside the Herald's guard. 
He plunges *The Shard* directly into the Herald's chest.

**SFX:**
*SKRRRKT!*

---

## PAGE 27
**PANEL 1:**
Kael isn't just stabbing it. He is channeling his own corrupted energy down the blade, feeding the Siphon its own power on an infinite feedback loop.

**KAEL (Screaming):**
CHOKE ON IT!

**PANEL 2:**
The Herald violently spasms. The core behind it begins to crack, unable to handle the feedback loop. 

**SFX:**
*KRAK-KRAK-KRAK!*

**PANEL 3 (Large Panel):**
The core shatters. A massive, silent explosion of white light consumes the entire page. 

---

## PAGE 28 (Epilogue)
**PANEL 1:**
Total silence. The dark, flooded streets of Noakhali Block at dawn. The AXIOM neon signs are completely dead. The grid is down.

**PANEL 2:**
Jamal and Ustad walk out into the street. The rain has stopped. They look up at the sky. 

**PANEL 3:**
Underground, at the Torii gate. Borkot’s blue optics flicker back on. 
Footsteps approach in the dark.

**PANEL 4:**
Kael walks out of the shadows. His armor is in shreds. He is bleeding, exhausted, but alive. 

**BORKOT:**
You came back.

**PANEL 5 (Final Panel):**
Close up on Kael’s face as he smirks. His eyes are no longer glowing. They are just dark brown. Human. 

**KAEL:**
Said I would.

**CAPTION:**
**THE END.**
