# IRONBLOOD: THE BARGE ENCOUNTER (Act II Scene)

**Scene:** *The Godfather of the Wet Bazaars*  
**Location:** The *Sultana* — Jahangir Bhai's floating luxury flagship barge, anchored in the flooded central canal of the Wet Bazaars.  
**Characters:** Kael, Jahangir Bhai, Jamal.  

---

## 🎬 SCENE START

**PANEL 1 (Establishing Shot):**
Outside, the monsoon rain lashes against the flooded canals. Hundreds of wooden sampans and floating market stalls glow under flickering green and violet neon signs. 
In the center floats the *Sultana*—a massive, three-story vintage paddle-wheel barge built from dark polished mahogany, guarded by men in tailored charcoal suits holding submachine guns.

**PANEL 2:**
Inside the private stateroom. Warm amber lamplight. A vintage brass gramophone crackles softly in the corner, playing a slow, smoky jazz-orchestra melody. Rain taps against stained-glass windows.

**PANEL 3:**
**JAHANGIR BHAI** sits behind a massive carved teakwood desk. 
He wears a pinstriped three-piece suit beneath a heavy black wool overcoat with a velvet collar. His dark fedora is tilted slightly forward. Gold signet rings gleam on his fingers as he clips the end of a thick Cuban cigar with a gold cutter.

**PANEL 4:**
The heavy oak doors creak open. 
**KAEL** steps inside, water dripping from his tactical coat, muddy boots squeaking on the Persian rug. *The Shard* rests heavily on his shoulder, its jagged edge catching the amber chandelier light. **JAMAL** slips in behind him, looking nervous.

**JAHANGIR BHAI:**
*(Without looking up, striking a wooden match against his desk)*  
*"You're tracking river mud on an antique rug, Kael."*

**KAEL:**
*"I didn't come to look at your carpets, Jahangir."*

**PANEL 5:**
Jahangir puffs on his cigar, sending a thick ring of blue-grey smoke toward the ceiling. He looks up, his calculating brown eyes locking onto Kael.

**JAHANGIR BHAI:**
*"Three years ago, you walked out of the Sky-Scrap carrying my manifest. I gave you the coordinates to Noakhali. I told you to find me when the shooting started. You took your time."*

**KAEL:**
*"I was busy bleeding."*

**JAHANGIR BHAI:**
*(Smiles thinly, pouring two crystal glasses of amber whiskey)*  
*"We all bleed, son. The trick is making sure someone pays for the towels. Sit down."*

**PANEL 6:**
Kael doesn't sit. He plants *The Shard* straight into the floorboards next to the desk with a heavy *THUD*.

**KAEL:**
*"I need your heavy sub-surface demolition charges. And the biometric passcodes for the Dubai Quarter watergates. Voss has reinforced the sky-spires."*

**JAHANGIR BHAI:**
*(Leans back in his leather armchair, swirling his glass)*  
*"Those charges cost twenty thousand credits each on the black market. The passcodes cost me two dead captains in the bay. Why should I give them to a broken soldier with a suicide wish?"*

**PANEL 7 (Close-Up on Kael):**
Kael leans forward over the desk, his green Siphon veins pulsing violently beneath his scarred skin, illuminating the table in an eerie green glow.

**KAEL:**
*"Because once I breach the Dubai Quarter, I'm going after Governor Mujib. And when Mujib's grid falls, AXIOM's automated tax scanners go dark across every canal in the country. You get your trade routes back. Unchecked. Untaxed. Forever."*

**PANEL 8 (Close-Up on Jahangir):**
Jahangir stops swirling his glass. He stares into Kael's glowing green eyes for three long seconds. The air in the room goes dead cold.

**JAHANGIR BHAI:**
*"...You have Mujib's pride, boy. But none of his patience. Do you have any idea what that man can do when he takes off his coat?"*

**KAEL:**
*"I'll find out."*

**PANEL 9:**
Jahangir chuckles—a low, gravelly sound. He reaches into a hidden drawer beneath the desk and slides a heavy, brass-encased cryptographic drive across the mahogany table, alongside a steel keycard.

**JAHANGIR BHAI:**
*"The watergate codes. The charges are loaded on a skiff tied to the aft deck. Consider it an investment."*

**PANEL 10:**
Kael picks up the drive and pulls *The Shard* free from the floor. He turns toward the door.

**JAHANGIR BHAI:**
*(Calling out as Kael reaches the exit)*  
*"Kael."*

**PANEL 11:**
Kael pauses at the threshold, turning his head.

**JAHANGIR BHAI:**
*(Taking a long pull from his cigar, face partially shadowed by his fedora)*  
*"When you stand in front of Mujib... tell him Jahangir says his nation was always too small for the both of us."*

**PANEL 12 (Final Shot):**
Kael nods once, stepping back into the torrential rain of the Wet Bazaars.

**CAPTION:**
*The deal was struck.*
*The gates to the capital were open.*
