# IRONBLOOD: FIRST BLOOD — Origin Story DLC

**Tagline:** *Before the block. Before the Siphon. There was the job that brought them together.*

---

## 🌍 SETTING & PREMISE

**Old Dhaka & The Sky-Scrap. 2067. Three years before the main game.**

AXIOM hasn't arrived in Noakhali yet. They're still consolidating power in the capital. Kael is still a district soldier — loyal, disciplined, following orders he hasn't started questioning yet. Borkot is still an active AXIOM combat unit, designation **AX-07**, patrolling the sealed sectors of Old Dhaka. The crew doesn't know each other. They have no reason to.

Until a job goes wrong.

**The Setup:** A black-market data broker in the Sky-Scrap hires five strangers independently for a heist — steal a classified AXIOM cargo manifest from a sealed server farm buried deep inside the Old Dhaka Core. The sealed city. The dead zone. The place where the ash never settled.

None of them know the others are coming. None of them know what the manifest actually contains. And none of them know that AXIOM sealed Old Dhaka for a reason — because something is living in the ash.

By the time they find each other, the job has already gone sideways. The only way out is together.

---

## 🗺️ THE TWO LOCATIONS

### THE OLD DHAKA CORE (Acts I & II)
**Aesthetic:** Strict **monochrome**. The entire zone is black, white, and thick grey ash-fog. Color doesn't exist here. The only light comes from the piercing **red optics** of ancient automated defense drones still patrolling the silent streets. Buildings are intact but completely abandoned — frozen in time, coated in pale ash. It is oppressively quiet. Sound is muffled. Footsteps echo wrong.

**Gameplay Feel:** Tense, slow, stealth-focused. Visibility is terrible. Enemies appear as red dots in the fog before you can see their shapes. The monochrome palette makes it feel like a completely different game.

---

### THE SKY-SCRAP (Act III)
**Aesthetic:** The polar opposite. A chaotic, **diesel-punk vertical shantytown** suspended thousands of feet in the air between ruined skyscraper peaks. Lashed together with heavy cables, scrap metal platforms, and massive rusted zeppelins. Everything is **warm, smoggy orange** — sunset haze, diesel exhaust, flickering barrel fires. Constant howling wind. Spinning propellers. Life is loud, dangerous, and vertical.

**Gameplay Feel:** Fast, frantic, vertical platforming. The platforms sway. The wind pushes you. Everything feels precarious and alive — the total opposite of Old Dhaka's suffocating silence.

## 👤 NEW CHARACTERS

### KPOP NINJA (Real Name: Unknown)
**Inspiration:** Gojo Satoru's exact aesthetic — but he's a long-range sniper, not a sorcerer.

**Look:** Tall, lean, impossibly stylish for someone living in a war zone. Wears a **skin-tight, high-collared black tactical bodysuit** with electric blue accent lines running down the arms and spine. A thick black blindfold covers his eyes at all times (he claims he "sees better without looking"). Silver-white hair, slicked back. Always smirking. Carries a heavily modified, matte-black **anti-materiel sniper rifle** nearly as tall as he is, slung casually over one shoulder.

**Personality:** Insufferably confident. Talks like he's narrating his own anime. Calls every shot before he takes it — and never misses. The crew can't stand him. They also can't survive without him. He treats the entire apocalypse like it's content for his nonexistent followers.

**Defining Lines:**
- *"I don't need to see you to hit you. I just need to know you exist."*
- *"Chat, we are SO winning this."* (said to nobody)
- *[After a perfect headshot from 800 meters]* *"Clip that."*

**Combat Role:** Overwatch. He never fights on the ground. He's always perched on the highest point available, calling out targets and dropping enemies before they reach the crew. In gameplay, he provides timed sniper support — red laser lines sweep the battlefield and eliminate marked targets.

### LILA (The Diva)
**Inspiration:** Mitsuri Kanroji's warmth and flamboyance meets a Bollywood action heroine.

**Look:** Tall, athletic, radiantly beautiful in a way that is completely absurd for a warzone. Long, thick hair dyed in a dramatic **gradient — deep magenta at the roots fading to pastel pink at the tips** (she maintains it religiously even during the apocalypse). Wears a **custom-fitted armored corset** over a flowing, torn combat sari in deep crimson and gold. Ornate gold bangles on both wrists that double as weighted throwing weapons. Thigh-high armored boots.

**Weapon:** A **flexible, whip-like sword** — a long, segmented blade that can extend and coil like a ribbon. She fights with elaborate, dance-like movements that look theatrical but are devastatingly precise.

**Personality:** Aggressively kind. Compliments everyone mid-combat. Cooks for the crew using whatever she can find (it's always surprisingly good). Has zero tolerance for cruelty — she joined the job because the broker told her AXIOM was hurting people in Old Dhaka. She would have done it for free. Falls in love with someone new every 48 hours (currently: KPop Ninja, who is completely oblivious).

**Defining Lines:**
- *"You're doing amazing, sweetie!"* (said to Kael while he's bleeding out)
- *"I made rice. With ACTUAL rice. Do you know how hard that was to find?"*
- *[To an AXIOM soldier, genuinely concerned]* *"Are you sure you want to do this? You look tired. Have you eaten today?"*
- *[0.3 seconds later, absolutely destroying said soldier]*

**Combat Role:** Mid-range crowd control. Her whip-sword has massive reach and sweeps through groups. She's the crew's area denial specialist — nothing gets past her without getting sliced.

## 📖 ORIGIN STORY — ACT BY ACT

### ACT I — THE ASH (Old Dhaka Core)

**Setup:** Five strangers enter the sealed Old Dhaka Core through different access points, each hired independently by the same anonymous broker.

- **KAEL** enters through the military checkpoint (he's still a soldier — he has clearance, but he's doing this off-book for the money).
- **JAMAL** sneaks in through the drainage pipes (he's 16, already running jobs for street bosses).
- **KPOP NINJA** is already inside, perched on a clock tower. He's been here for two days. He's bored.
- **LILA** walks in through the front gate. The defense drones scan her. She smiles at them. They let her pass. (Nobody understands how.)
- **USTAD** is hired separately as the tech specialist to crack the server farm's encryption.

**Mission 1: THE DEAD STREETS**
Kael moves through the ash-choked streets alone. Monochrome palette. Red drone eyes in the fog. Tense, quiet. He reaches the server farm entrance and finds it's already been breached — someone cut through the lock with a plasma torch.

Inside, he finds Jamal trying to hack a terminal and failing spectacularly.

**JAMAL:**
*"I'm not stealing. I'm... redistributing data. Who are you?"*

**KAEL:**
*"The guy with the bigger knife. Move."*

**Mission 2: THE SERVER FARM**
Kael and Jamal push deeper. The facility is enormous and dark. They trigger an alarm. Ancient defense drones activate — red eyes flooding the corridors. They're pinned down.

Then, one by one, the drones' heads explode. Clean shots. From somewhere they can't see.

**KPOP NINJA (over a hijacked intercom, casually):**
*"You're welcome. Third floor, northeast stairwell. I'll keep the lights on."*

They find KPop Ninja on the roof, lying on his stomach behind his massive rifle, blindfold on, smirking.

**KAEL:**
*"You can't even see."*

**KPOP NINJA:**
*"And yet here we are."*

---

### ACT II — THE SIGNAL (Old Dhaka Core — Deep)

**Mission 3: THE ARCHIVE**
The crew — now four — descends into the sub-levels of the server farm. Ustad is already there, elbow-deep in wiring, trying to extract the manifest data. He's panicking because the encryption is military-grade and he's running out of time.

**USTAD:**
*"If it has wires, I can make it explode. If it doesn't... give me a minute. Okay, maybe ten minutes. Maybe stop looking at me."*

While Ustad works, something moves in the deep ash outside the facility. The ground shakes. A low, subsonic hum fills the air. The drones in the facility stop attacking the crew — and start retreating.

**KAEL:**
*"Why are they running?"*

**KPOP NINJA (suddenly serious for the first time):**
*"Because something worse just woke up."*

**Boss: THE CUSTODIAN** — The thing AXIOM sealed Old Dhaka to contain. An ancient automated defense system that has been running for decades without maintenance, absorbing the city's infrastructure into itself. It's a massive, spider-like construct made of fused concrete, rebar, and old military hardware, dragging itself through the ash on eight uneven legs. Its "face" is a cluster of mismatched red camera lenses.

The crew fights it together for the first time. It's messy, uncoordinated, and chaotic. Kael charges in alone and gets thrown through a wall. KPop Ninja can't get a clean shot because Lila keeps dancing into his sightline. Jamal accidentally sets Ustad's toolkit on fire.

But they win. Barely.

**LILA (bandaging Kael's arm, beaming):**
*"See? We make a great team!"*

**KAEL (bleeding):**
*"We absolutely do not."*

---

### ACT III — THE ESCAPE (The Sky-Scrap)

**Mission 4: THE ASCENT**
The manifest data reveals something none of them expected — AXIOM is planning to move into Noakhali District. A full military occupation disguised as infrastructure development. The broker who hired them? Already dead. AXIOM killed him to bury the manifest.

Now AXIOM knows someone pulled the data. Old Dhaka is being flooded with troops. The only escape route is UP — through the vertical shantytown called **The Sky-Scrap**, suspended between the skyscraper peaks above the sealed city.

The aesthetic explodes from monochrome silence into warm, chaotic, diesel-punk orange. Wind howls. Platforms sway. Zeppelins groan overhead. The crew has to climb.

**KPOP NINJA:**
*"Vertical extraction. My favorite. Chat, this is going to be incredible."*

**JAMAL:**
*"WHO ARE YOU TALKING TO?!"*

**Mission 5: THE PEAK**
AXIOM deploys a gunship. The crew is pinned on a swaying platform hundreds of meters above the ground. Ferro-style heavy combat meets desperate vertical platforming.

This is where the crew clicks. Kael leads. Ustad rigs the zeppelin cables to create ziplines. KPop Ninja picks off gunship turrets from an impossible angle. Lila sweeps the boarding soldiers off the platforms with her whip-sword. Jamal — the fastest — runs the final cable to the escape zeppelin.

**Final Boss: AXIOM HUNTER DRONE** — A massive, autonomous attack helicopter drone that pursues them through the Sky-Scrap. The crew fights it while climbing, platform by platform, as the structure collapses beneath them.

## ⚙️ IMPLEMENTATION PLAN — OPEN QUESTIONS

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These are questions we need to resolve before building the DLC into the engine.

### Engine Architecture
1. **How do we handle the monochrome shader?** Old Dhaka Core needs to render the entire game in greyscale with only red accents. Options:
   - A. Post-processing filter on the canvas (`ctx.filter = 'grayscale(1)'`) with red sprites exempted
   - B. Alternate color palettes baked into the tileset/sprite assets themselves
   - C. A shader-like approach using `getImageData` to desaturate per-frame

2. **Vertical scrolling for Sky-Scrap?** The main game scrolls horizontally. The Sky-Scrap needs vertical platforming. Options:
   - A. Rotate the tilemap concept (columns become the scroll axis)
   - B. Keep horizontal scrolling but make the levels very tall with vertical camera tracking
   - C. Hybrid — some sections scroll horizontally, some vertically

3. **New characters as playable or NPC support?**
   - KPop Ninja as a sniper — do we make him a timed event (red laser → auto-kill) or player-controlled?
   - Lila — playable swap character with different moveset, or NPC ally?

4. **DLC as separate mission set or integrated?** 
   - A. Separate "FIRST BLOOD" option on the title screen (cleanest)
   - B. Unlocked after completing the main game
   - C. Prequel missions woven into the existing mission select

### Pending from Main Game
5. **Step 2 (Document Collectibles)** — Still not built. Should we finish the main game's Phase 1 steps before starting DLC content?
6. **Step 3 (Boss Class)** — The Custodian and the Hunter Drone both need the boss fight framework.

> [!TIP]
> My recommendation: Finish **Steps 2 and 3** of the main game engine first. The Document system and Boss class are needed by both the main game AND this DLC. Then we can build the DLC-specific features (monochrome filter, vertical scroll) on top of a stable base.
