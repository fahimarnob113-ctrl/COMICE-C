# IRONBLOOD — Character Design & Generation Prompts
*A ready-to-copy guide for generating character concept art and sprite sheets in Midjourney, DALL-E, or Stable Diffusion.*

---

## 🎨 GLOBAL PROMPT MODIFIERS (Add to the end of every prompt)
> **For Sprite Sheets:** `sprite sheet, 16-bit pixel art, late 90s arcade fighting game style, Capcom CP System II, Metal Slug aesthetic, flat colors, no anti-aliasing, solid background, full body, character design, sharp pixel edges`
> 
> **For Concept Art:** `dark cyberpunk, 2070 Bangladesh, gritty, heavy contrast, Yoji Shinkawa style mixed with 90s anime, detailed character concept art, cinematic lighting`

## 1. KAEL (The Protagonist)
**Outfit & Vibe:** Logan/Punisher build. Charcoal tactical gear, cracked AXIOM chest plate spray-painted with a jagged X. Worn heavy boots. Glowing teal-green vein scars on his left arm. Carries an obscenely large, crude single-edged cleaver sword strapped diagonally across his back. Dark, unkempt hair, rough beard, haunted eyes.

**Copy/Paste Prompt:**
> `Character concept art, KAEL. A rugged, muscular man with dark unkempt hair and a rough beard. Wearing charcoal tactical gear and a cracked corporate chest plate spray-painted with a jagged X. Glowing teal-green energy veins pulsing on his left arm. He carries an obscenely massive, crude single-edged iron cleaver sword strapped to his back. Gritty cyberpunk style, neon lighting, dark and moody, fighting game protagonist.`
