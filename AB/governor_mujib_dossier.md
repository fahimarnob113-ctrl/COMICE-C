## 🏛️ 1. LORE & CHARACTER BACKGROUND

### The Bloodline & The Burden
Governor Mujib is a direct descendant of the Father of the Nation, carrying the family's iconic stature, thunderous voice, and unyielding will into the flooded apocalypse of 2070. Where his ancestor fought to birth a nation, Governor Mujib fights a desperate war to keep that nation from being wiped off the map by climate disaster and corporate colonization.

When the sea levels rose and millions drowned in the chaos, Mujib stepped into the breach. He made the cold, agonizing calculation to sign the **Pact of Sovereignty** with AXIOM Corporation:
- **The Deal:** AXIOM got extraction access to deep subterranean energy (The Siphon). In return, Mujib extracted billions in advanced military tech, flood dikes, and energy infrastructure to keep thirty million citizens alive on dry ground.
- **His Philosophy:** *"A hungry man does not care about democracy. He cares about dry floorboards, clean water, and walls that hold back the storm. I gave them a country when nature tried to drown us. History can judge my methods; I kept the flame burning."*

### He Answers to No One
Mujib is not an AXIOM puppet. He plays a ruthless, multi-tiered game of chess against corporate boardrooms, underworld crime syndicates like Jahangir Bhai, and street militias. He uses AXIOM's heavy dreadnoughts to protect his borders, all while preparing for the day the country can purge the foreign corporations entirely.
