# IRONBLOOD: THE ANCIENT SIPHON MYTHOS

**Nature:** Primordial Living Subterranean Organ of Earth  
**Age:** Estimated 450 Million Years (Pre-Human)  
**Location:** The Subterranean Mantle beneath the Bengal Delta  

---

## 🌍 1. WHAT THE SIPHON TRULY IS

The Siphon is neither an alien invasion nor a toxic chemical spill. 

It is an **ancient, primordial biological organ of the planet Earth itself**—a massive subterranean bio-magnetic neural wellspring that has rested quietly beneath the shifting silt and rivers of the Bengal Delta for hundreds of millions of years.

In ancient Vedic and prehistoric oral traditions, it was whispered of as *"The Slumbering Heart of the Tides"*—a force that regulated the monsoon cycles, the fertility of the delta's soil, and the geomagnetic balance of the hemisphere. It is neither good nor evil; it is raw planetary life.

---

## ⚡ 2. THE WOUND & THE AWAKENING

For millennia, the Siphon slept in equilibrium with the surface. 

Then came the corporate era:
- In 2055, AXIOM Corporation deployed deep-mantle tectonic drills, piercing five miles beneath the delta looking for rare-earth energy conduits.
- They didn't drill into rock. **They punctured a living planetary nervous system.**

The pain of that wound sent shockwaves through the tectonic plates, triggering the catastrophic sea-level floods that drowned southern Bangladesh. Leaking raw, pressurized bio-energy into the water tables, the Siphon awoke in agony and self-defense.
