# IRONBLOOD: KAEL — The Iron Hound

**Name:** **KAEL** (The Defiant / Subject-01)  
**Inspiration:** Wolverine / The Punisher (Frank Castle) / Deadpool (haunted ex-soldier archetype)  
**Status:** Supreme Commander of the Noakhali Resistance  
**Weapon:** *The Shard* (Massive jagged cleaver sword resonating with Siphon energy)  

---

## 📜 1. THE TRAGIC PAST: WHAT KAEL LOST

Before the cleaver, the neon green scars, and the resistance, Kael was an elite frontline sergeant in the Coastal Defense Guard. He fought for peace and believed in the system.

AXIOM's arrival took everything from him in two brutal strikes:
1. **The Loss of His Family (Wife & Child):** During the initial corporate takeover of the southern coastal district, AXIOM privatized the flood dikes. When the monsoons spiked, AXIOM closed the emergency gates to protect their automated drill platforms, submerging the residential district. Kael's wife and 5-year-old daughter drowned trapped in their own home.
2. **The Loss of His Brother-in-Arms:** When Kael and his squad commander confronted the corporate overseers, his commander—his closest mentor and brother—was executed on the spot by an AXIOM firing squad. Kael was chained, tortured, and thrown into a subterranean containment bunker near the Siphon core.

He didn't die. He absorbed the raw, pulsing energy of the Siphon into his bloodstream, broke his chains with bare hands, seized a slab of broken blast-door steel (*The Shard*), and carved his way to freedom.

---

## 🐺 2. PERSONALITY: THE HAUNTED VETERAN

- **The Frank Castle Drive:** Kael is not fighting for glory, money, or a political seat. He is a man with nothing left to lose. He walks toward gunfire with the cold, methodical certainty of a man whose soul died years ago.
- **The Wolverine Burden:** The Siphon energy in his blood gives him explosive kinetic strength and regeneration, but it constantly whispers violently in his mind. He fights a continuous internal battle to avoid becoming a mindless monster.
