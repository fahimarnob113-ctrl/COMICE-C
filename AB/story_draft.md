## 🌍 WORLD BACKGROUND

### The Setting
**Noakhali District, Bangladesh. 2070.**
The world is recognisably Bangladesh, but fundamentally altered by decades of climate change.
The sea levels rose; Noakhali is now heavily waterlogged, built on stilts and elevated walkways
above flooded lower streets. It's overlaid with fifty years of uneven tech. Solar mesh on every roof.
Drone delivery lanes above the markets. Corporate English on every sign beside the Bengali.
The people are poorer than the technology around them suggests. That gap is where AXIOM operates.

AXIOM Corporation — no flag, no country, just capital — moved in seven years ago under a
development contract. Roads, power grid upgrades, connectivity infrastructure. The district
welcomed them. Jobs came. Then the checkpoints came. Then the army.

The official story: security for AXIOM's infrastructure investment.
The real reason: a geological survey found something beneath the district.
Something old. Something that generates energy unlike anything measurable by 2070 instruments.
They called it **The Siphon**.

### The Siphon
Not a machine. Not a creature. An **entity** — ancient beyond categorisation, buried, partially dormant.
AXIOM has been slowly tapping its energy output to power their control grid across the district.
The problem: tapping it is waking it up.
The Hollow Vein creatures aren't AXIOM's weapons — they're the Siphon's immune system,
attacking anything it registers as a sustained threat. Including AXIOM.
AXIOM knows this. They're gambling they can extract what they need before it fully wakes.
They are wrong.

**What the Siphon is not**: evil. It has no malice. It has no agenda.
It is simply vast, old, and now disturbed. It remembers everything it has ever touched.
That makes it more dangerous than a villain would be.

### AXIOM Corporation
Neutral multinational. No nationality. No ideology beyond extraction and return-on-investment.
Not cartoonishly evil — functionally evil. Bureaucratic, efficient, believes its own mission statement.
Their soldiers follow orders because orders make sense in their system.
Their commanders believe in the cause because the cause pays well and looks legitimate on paper.
This makes them more dangerous than a simple villain.
There is no one to defeat. There is only a system to break.

## 👤 CHARACTERS

### KAEL (Player)
Former district soldier. Left after the occupation began — couldn't serve two masters.
Has been fighting AXIOM informally for months, alone.
CIPHER found him. Gave him direction.
**Motivation**: The block is his home. He's not saving the world. He's saving his street.
**Internal conflict**: The longer he stays near the Siphon, the more its energy bleeds into him.
His eyes glow brighter. His rage comes faster. He doesn't know if that's power or corruption.
**Design**: Charcoal tactical gear, neon green energy scars, large cleaver, beard, haunted eyes.

### CIPHER
**Identity: permanently unknown. Never revealed. Not even hinted at.**
A voice. Encrypted audio only. No face, no name, no origin confirmed.
Claims to be a former AXIOM data analyst who found the Siphon files and went underground.
May be telling the truth. May not be.
Runs a resistance network from a location no one has ever seen.
**Voice**: Dry, precise, occasionally dark humour. Never panics. Even when they should.
**Secret**: CIPHER has seen the full Siphon projection data. They know how this ends.
They haven't told KAEL. The game ends before we find out why.
**Rule**: No reveal. No face. No body. The mystery is the character.

### BORKOT
**Role**: Heavy muscle. The moral center.
Former AXIOM military-grade combat robot — taller than KAEL by a head.
Reprogrammed by the resistance, now self-aware. Stripped of corporate insignia, battered and
repainted matte black and dark red. One arm rebuilt from scavenged parts.
**Personality**: Speaks simply. Thinks deeply. Cannot lie, which is why KAEL trusts him most.
**Line that defines him**: *"You came back. That is within acceptable parameters of loyalty."*

### JAMAL
Young. Too young for this. District runner — fastest in the block.
Brings CIPHER's instructions to KAEL physically when the signal is jammed.
Comic relief that earns it. Never on the front line by choice.
**Line that defines him**: *"I ran fourteen blocks with this message. Fourteen. You better read it."*

### MASUD
The heavy weapons specialist. Ex-special forces, covered in salvaged AXIOM tech armor.
Operates a gatling-mounted shoulder rig. Dry, deadpan humor.
Never fights for free, except when it comes to the block.
**Line that defines him**: *"I don't pick sides. I pick survivors."*

### USTAD
The crew's heart and technician. Short, stocky, wears a worn work-helmet.
Former city maintenance worker who knows every flooded tunnel and duct in the district.
Opens locked routes and bypasses AXIOM systems.
**Line that defines him**: *"If it has wires, I can make it explode. If it doesn't... give me a minute."*

### JAHANGIR BHAI
Surface crime lord. The only clean one in the room, wearing a tailored coat.
Controls what remains of organized trade on the surface. Walks with a cane that conceals a blade.
Provides the crew with safehouses, intel, and resources. Nobody absorbs Jahangir Bhai.
**Line that defines him**: *"I don't have enemies. I have people who haven't paid me yet."*

### MIRJA ABBAS
The flashy one. Ex-resistance performer who hijacked AXIOM's broadcast feeds.
Wears theatrical scavenged armor, dual oversized modified rebar cleavers.
Spectacle and speed. Distracts entire squads with his sheer presence.
**Line that defines him**: *"If I die today, I die looking magnificent."*

### RUSSIAN JAHANGIR
The chaos agent. Grapple-hook rig, always carrying too many explosives.
No one knows his real origin, but he speaks Bengali with a bizarre Russian accent.
Drops in when things need to explode. Calls Jahangir Bhai his "brother from another mother."
**Line that defines him**: *"This wall offends me. I will remove it."*

### THE WARDEN (Act I Boss)
Iron Collar regional enforcer. Not a monster — a professional.
Has been running the Noakhali checkpoint system for two years.
Knows the names of every person he's displaced. Keeps physical records in a notebook.
Believes in order above all else. AXIOM's order is the only order he recognises.
**Design** ✅ LOCKED:
- Full heavy iron armour — dark rust-brown, riveted plates
- Half-mask on the left side of his face. Right side exposed: one scarred eye, weathered skin
- Chain whip weapon — iron links, heavy, devastating swing radius
- Large and imposing — wider and taller than any grunt
- Moves slowly but with absolute certainty
**Defeat line**: *"You won a block. They own the grid. You don't understand what you've started."*

### COMMANDANT VOSS (Act II Boss)
AXIOM's tactical commander. Former military, heavily augmented post-service.
Neural link to AXIOM's tactical network — processes battlefield data faster than human cognition.
Not cruel. Methodical. Treats KAEL as an interesting tactical problem.
His personal logs show he tried to shut down the extraction six months ago. Was overruled.
**Design** ✅ LOCKED:
- Sleek AXIOM blue exoskeleton — clean lines, no rust, no wear (maintained)
- Neural implants at both temples — visible silver nodes with thin wire traces across the skull
- Visor across both eyes — displays tactical data, glows faint blue
- Moves with inhuman precision — no wasted motion, no tell before an attack
- Smaller than The Warden. More dangerous for it.
**Defeat line**: *"The Siphon doesn't care about either of us. You know that."*

### THE SIPHON HERALD (Act III Final Boss)
Not a person. A fragment of the Siphon given temporary physical form — the first coherent
shape it has made. Assembled, unconsciously, from the physical matter of people who got too close.
Speaks in broken absorbed phrases — memories of the people it was made from.
**Design** ✅ LOCKED:
- Elongated humanoid — too tall, wrong proportions, arms too long
- Four arms — two primary (forward), two secondary (trailing behind like broken wings)
- Dark teal-black surface — matte, textured like dried organic matter
- Teal energy visible through cracks across the entire body — like lava, but cold-light
- Hollow where a face should be. Two points of teal light where eyes would be.
- No mouth visible. The voice comes from everywhere.
**Lines during fight**: *"...you are already inside..."* / *"...the block... the block is us..."* / *"...don't leave..."*

### ACT II — THE FACILITY

#### Pre-Mission Cutscene (art panel — Dubai Quarter neon signs, AXIOM armoured trucks)
> **CIPHER:** *"Dubai Quarter. AXIOM's forward base. This is where they coordinate
> the feed to the Siphon — energy extraction, data relay, personnel.
> Shut it down and they lose their connection to the extraction grid.
> The Hollow Vein activity triples every week. They're running out of time.
> So are we."*

#### Mission 3: DUBAI QUARTER
*Infiltrate the AXIOM forward base. The Hollow Vein creatures appear here — attacking both KAEL and AXIOM soldiers.*

**CIPHER (on first Hollow Vein encounter):**
> *"Those aren't AXIOM weapons. They're coming from below.
> The Siphon's immune system. It attacks anything it registers as a threat.
> Apparently you qualify. So does AXIOM.
> That should tell you something about where this is heading."*

**JAMAL (intercepted transmission, mid-mission):**
> *"KAEL. Fourteen blocks to get this to CIPHER. Fourteen.
> Masud says the southern access tunnel is clear. Use it."*

#### Post-Mission 3 / Pre-Mission 4 Cutscene (art panel — feed towers against dark sky, energy pulses visible)
> **CIPHER:** *"Four feed towers. Bring them down and AXIOM loses the extraction link.
> The Siphon will stabilise. Temporarily.
> Commandant Voss will be waiting. He already knows you're coming.
> He's been watching since the signal tower."*

#### Mission 4: THE SIGNAL SPINE
*Destroy the four feed towers across the mission. Heaviest resistance yet.*
*(MASUD sets up on a distant ridge, providing heavy cover fire and ripping through AXIOM armor with his gatling rig.)*

**MASUD (radioing in):**
> *"I don't work for free, KAEL. But for these towers... I'll make an exception. Keep them busy. I'll crack the armor."*

**CIPHER (on destroying each tower):**
> Tower 1: *"One down. The pulse just changed frequency. It felt that."*
> Tower 2: *"Two. AXIOM's scrambling. Voss just mobilised."*
> Tower 3: *"Three. The Hollow Vein activity just spiked. It's confused."*
> Tower 4: *"Four. The link is broken. Now run."*

#### Boss: COMMANDANT VOSS
*Three-phase fight. Each phase sees Voss adapt tactics via neural link.*

**Pre-fight:**
> **VOSS:** *"KAEL. I've been tracking you since Sector 7.
> You're not an insurgent. You're a man protecting his home.
> I respect that. It doesn't change what happens next."*

**Phase 2 (tactical adaptation):**
> **VOSS:** *"Adjusting. Your patterns are predictable at this point."*

**Phase 3 (below 25% HP, link degrading):**
> **VOSS:** *"The link is... the data is... [static] ...the Siphon doesn't care about either of us.
> You know that, don't you?"*

**Defeat:**
> **VOSS:** *"The extraction link is broken. The Siphon will now wake on its own schedule.
> Not AXIOM's. Not yours. Its own.
> You have hours. Maybe less."*

#### Act II End Cutscene (art panel — ground cracking, teal light pulsing up through earth)
> **CIPHER:** *"Voss was right. Without the controlled extraction, the Siphon is waking
> faster — not slower. We bought time. Minutes, not days.
> The core is accessible from the underground access point beneath the district.
> KAEL — what you do down there is your call. I'll guide you in.
> I won't tell you what to do when you find it."*
>
> **BORKOT:** *"I'm going with you."*
>
> **CIPHER:** *"BORKOT. Your injury—"*
>
> **BORKOT:** *"I'm going."*

### ACT III — THE DEEP

#### Pre-Mission Cutscene (art panel — underground flooded tunnel, bioluminescent teal moss)
> **CIPHER:** *"The outer shell of the Siphon starts here. We call it the Seattle Depths.
> Perpetual rain from old broken water infrastructure.
> Hollow Vein creatures are dense below this point.
> AXIOM has abandoned the extraction site — their personnel pulled out an hour ago.
> Whatever's down here, they decided it wasn't worth staying for.
> That should concern you."*

#### Mission 5: SEATTLE DEPTHS
*Descend through the Siphon's flooded outer shell. Hollow Vein creatures everywhere. Water drips constantly from above, pooling around ancient machinery.*
*(Near the end of the descent, the path is completely blocked by hardened Siphon-matter. RUSSIAN JAHANGIR drops in from a ventilation shaft above, grappling onto a rusted pipe.)*

**RUSSIAN JAHANGIR:**
> *"This wall offends me. I will remove it. Stand back, brother!"*
*(He detonates a massive shaped charge, blowing a hole straight down into the Deep Wild, before grappling back up into the darkness.)*

**CIPHER (on entering the deep zone):**
> *"The Siphon's structure is... absorbing the architecture around it.
> The walls aren't stone. They're older than stone.
> Keep moving. Don't stop in one place long enough to let it register you."*

**Ambient Siphon voice (fragments, as KAEL descends):**
> *"...the block..."*
> *"...you know this place..."*
> *"...we remember you..."*

#### Mission 6: THE DEEP WILD (Core Approach)
*Final corridor. The environment shifts into something ancient — corrupted Japanese aesthetics, dark torii gates overgrown with teal Siphon-flora. A deep bass rumble mixed with the sound of a lone koto string echoing in the dark. No enemies — just the Siphon's presence, growing heavier.*
*BORKOT can't go further. He stays at the last safe point.*

**BORKOT (at the threshold):**
> *"This is as far as my chassis can go. The ground here — it rejects my mechanics—"*
> *"Go. I'll hold this position. Come back."*

**CIPHER (final transmission before core):**
> *"KAEL. When you reach the core — there's something I didn't tell you.
> The Siphon can be destroyed. I know how.
> But I've also seen what its energy does to a person who carries it long enough.
> Your eyes. Your rage. That's not corruption. That's integration.
> You're already part of it. Whether you destroy it or use it against itself —
> that's a choice only you can make.
> I'll be here. Whatever you decide."*

## 🎬 STORY / LORE INSPIRATIONS

### Games
| Game | What It Taught Us |
|---|---|
| **Metal Gear Solid 2** | A villain who is right about the wrong things. The Warden's records. Voss's resignation. |
| **Disco Elysium** | A city as a character. Noakhali is not just a setting — it has memory. |
| **Hollow Knight** | An ancient entity that isn't evil — just vast. The Siphon has no malice. |
| **Hades** | Short playtime, enormous lore density. Story delivered in fragments per run. |
| **Transistor** | A silent protagonist who speaks once. KAEL's single line in Ending A. |
| **Spec Ops: The Line** | Moral weight in a genre that usually ignores it. |
| **Final Fight / SOR4** | Structural blueprint — but the story here goes deeper than the genre usually allows. |

### Films
| Film | What It Taught Us |
|---|---|
| **Sicario** | Men following orders into moral grey zones. AXIOM's soldiers are Sicario soldiers. |
| **Annihilation** | An entity that isn't hostile — just fundamentally alien. The Siphon's nature. |
| **Memories of Murder** | A specific place carrying the weight of specific violence. Noakhali does this. |
| **Children of Men** | Hope in a world that has given up on it. The signal tower moment. |
| **Beasts of No Nation** | Conflict that is intimate, not epic. BORKOT and KAEL. |
| **Parasite** | Class, space, and what lives underneath. Literally — what lives underneath. |

### Literature
| Work | What It Taught Us |
|---|---|
| **Borne — Jeff VanderMeer** | A created thing that becomes more than its creators intended. |
| **The Dispossessed — Ursula K. Le Guin** | Two systems, neither fully right. AXIOM isn't lying about development. |
| **Underground Railroad — Colson Whitehead** | A network of ordinary people doing extraordinary things quietly. CIPHER's network. |

## ✏️ HOW TO EDIT DIALOGUE (Quick Reference)

All dialogue lives in **`config.js`** under `dialogue.cipher[]`.

**To change a CIPHER line:**
1. Open `config.js`
2. Find the `trigger` matching the mission ID (e.g., `m01_start`)
3. Change the `text` string
4. Save → refresh browser. Done.

**To add a new line to a mission:**
```js
{
  trigger:  'm01_start',   // when it fires
  speaker:  'CIPHER',      // name shown in dialogue box
  text:     'Your new line here.',
},
```

**To add a second transmission to the same trigger:**
Just add another object with the same `trigger` value. They play in sequence.

**Adaptive variants** (planned for Phase 1 build):
Will use `trigger: 'm01_start_confident'` and `trigger: 'm01_start_worried'`
based on the CIPHER confidence flag. Text in this document has both variants written —
they'll be wired in during the build phase.
