### PAGE 1: THE GLASS DOME AT DUSK

**PANEL 1 (High Angle Establishing Splash):**
Interior of the Grand Pavilion. A massive circular glass dome spans overhead. Outside, a violent monsoon thunderstorm rages across the elevated Dubai Quarter; lightning illuminates the rain-streaked glass. 
Inside, polished white marble floors are lined with deep blood-red ceremonial carpets and towering classical columns. 
At the far end, facing the storm with his back turned, stands **GOVERNOR MUJIB**.

**PANEL 2 (Floor Level Walk-In):**
The heavy brass doors behind Kael groan open. Kael walks down the red carpet. *The Shard* drags on the marble floor with a sharp metallic scrape. 
*SFX:* `SKRRRRRRRRRT...`

**PANEL 3 (Medium Shot - Mujib's Silhouette):**
Governor Mujib does not turn around. He is tall, broad-shouldered, and imposing. He wears his bespoke high-collared black **Mujib Coat** with crimson silk lining and gold-braided command aiguillettes draped over his left shoulder. 
He holds a polished briarwood cyber-pipe to his lips. A soft ring of amber-gold smoke drifts into the air.

**GOVERNOR MUJIB:**
*(Voice deep, booming, resonant like rolling thunder)*  
*"Thirty years ago, Kael, this entire delta was drowning. Thirty million souls were eating rats on submerged rooftops, begging for dry ground. Do you know who gave them walls to hold back the sea?"*

**PANEL 4 (Kael's Response):**
Kael stops ten paces away, his cleaver resting in his scarred hands, neon-green veins pulsing violently up his neck.

**KAEL:**
*"You made a deal with AXIOM, Governor. You sold our soil to foreign suits for drills and laser towers."*

**PANEL 5 (The Turn - Iconic Face Reveal):**
Governor Mujib slowly turns to face Kael. 
His face has the unmistakable, commanding likeness of **Sheikh Mujibur Rahman**—slicked-back grey-streaked hair, thick black vintage horn-rimmed glasses, and an authoritative mustache. His piercing eyes lock onto Kael through the frames.

**GOVERNOR MUJIB:**
*"A hungry man does not care about freedom, son. He cares about bread, clean water, and a roof that doesn't collapse under the monsoon. AXIOM gave us technology. I gave us a nation."*

**KAEL:**
*"You gave us a cage."*

**GOVERNOR MUJIB:**
*(Chuckles deeply, placing his pipe onto a marble pedestal)*  
*"Then let me show you what it takes to build one."*

### PAGE 2: THE ARMED MANDATE (Phase 1 — The State Decree)

**PANEL 1 (The Sovereign's Gesture):**
Governor Mujib raises his right hand, making a slight flick with his fingers. Biometric holographic rings project around his wrist.  
*SFX:* `BZZZZZT-HUUUUM!`

**PANEL 2 (Laser Grid Barrage):**
Across the marble pavilion, thirty red targeting lasers paint the floor in a crisscross grid. From the dreadnought’s exterior turrets, high-caliber kinetic artillery shells shatter through the outer parapet, exploding across the hall in blinding fireballs!  
*SFX:* `BOOM-BOOM-BOOM!`

**PANEL 3 (Acrobatic Cleaver Sprint):**
Kael sprints through the barrage, zigzagging between explosions. He vaults over a shattered marble pillar, using the flat of *The Shard* to deflect shrapnel as rain pours through broken glass.

**PANEL 4 (The Red Directorate Shock):**
Two elite **Red Directorate Praetorian Enforcers** in heavy power armor drop from the ceiling, slamming heavy riot shields into Kael's flank and charging shock lances!

**PANEL 5 (Counter-Cleave):**
Kael twists in mid-air, channeling green Siphon energy into his cleaver with a roar. He slices through both shock lances in a single horizontal arc, kicking one enforcer into the wall and shattering the second shield with a spinning cleaver chop!  
*SFX:* `KRAAA-SHZTTTTT!`

**PANEL 6 (Point-Blank Draw):**
Kael lunges straight for Governor Mujib. Without flinching, Mujib draws his gold-inlaid heavy magnum revolver (*"The Mandate"*) from his waist with blinding sub-second speed, aiming dead-center at Kael’s forehead!

**GOVERNOR MUJIB:**
*"Dismissed, soldier."*  
*SFX:* `KRA-THOOOOOM!`

### PAGE 3: TITANIUM AND BONE (Phase 2 — The Iron Will)

**PANEL 1 (The Bullet Parry):**
In 0.05 seconds, Kael angles *The Shard* in front of his face. The gold magnum slug strikes the center of the blade with a deafening *CLANG*, sparking a shockwave that slides Kael backwards across the carpet, tearing the fabric.  
*SFX:* `CLAAANNNG-SKRRRRT!`

**PANEL 2 (The Coat Unbuttons):**
Governor Mujib holsters his magnum and slowly unbuttons the front of his black Mujib coat. Beneath his white formal tunic, a **sub-dermal titanium combat spine rig** hums to life, glowing with deep crimson energy lines along his vertebrae and shoulders.

**GOVERNOR MUJIB:**
*"You think thirty years in office made me soft, boy? I took this country from the sea with my bare hands."*

**PANEL 3 (Explosive Speed Lunge):**
Mujib vanishes in a blur of motion. He reappears directly in front of Kael—moving faster than any human his age should—and drives a heavy palm strike directly into Kael's chestplate!  
*SFX:* `DOOOOM-CRACK!`

**PANEL 4 (The Shockwave):**
The kinetic impact shatters Kael's chest armor, launching Kael horizontally across the pavilion. He crashes through a reinforced stone pillar, coughing blood as masonry rains down.

**PANEL 5 (Brutal Ground Clash):**
Before Kael can rise, Mujib is already above him, delivering a devastating downward heel stomp. Kael rolls frantically to the side; Mujib’s boot obliterates the marble floor into a crater of pulverized stone!  
*SFX:* `KRA-BOOOOOOOM!`

### PAGE 4: THE SOVEREIGN PRESSURE (Phase 3 — Darth Vader-Tier Gravity)

**PANEL 1 (Extreme Tension - The Awakening of Authority):**
Governor Mujib stands at the center of the ruined pavilion. Lightning strikes behind him. He extends both arms outward, palms open. His crimson sub-dermal rig flares to maximum output, harmonizing with the dreadnought’s primary core.

**PANEL 2 (The Gravitational Drop):**
**The Sovereign Pressure** unleashes. The air pressure in the room multiplies by fifty. The remaining glass in the dome violently shatters into millions of falling shards that suspend frozen in mid-air! Heavy marble blocks begin floating off the ground, vibrating under intense gravitational distortion.  
*SFX:* `WUUUUUUURN-ZZZZZZT...`

**GOVERNOR MUJIB:**
*(Voice echoing across the storm, resonating with sheer authority)*  
*"You cannot cleave an empire, Kael! Kneel before the mandate!"*

**PANEL 3 (Kael Pinned):**
The crushing gravitational wave strikes Kael like a falling mountain. Kael is forced onto one knee, the stone cracking beneath him. His bones groan; blood drips from his nose. *The Shard* is pinned flat against the floor, too heavy to lift.

**PANEL 4 (Memory & Enlightenment):**
Kael closes his eyes. In the darkness, he hears **Master Diganta's** voice from the Drowned Archive:  
> *"The storm does not hate the tree it uproots... You cannot conquer a melody with a hammer, Kael. Become the living frequency."*

**PANEL 5 (The Breakthrough):**
Kael’s eyes snap open, blazing with an unholy, pure celestial **teal Siphon resonance**. He stops resisting the gravity—he harmonizes with it. The green veins across his skin ignite into blinding teal fire. With a thunderous roar, Kael rises to his feet, lifting *The Shard* with one arm as golden-teal shockwaves shatter Mujib’s gravitational hold!

### PAGE 5: THE FINAL CLASH & THE SURVIVAL

**PANEL 1 (Full Page Climax Strike):**
Kael and Governor Mujib collide in the center of the floating glass pavilion at hypersonic speed. 
- Mujib strikes with an all-out crimson kinetic palm thrust aiming to crush Kael’s heart.
- Kael executes a blinding diagonal upward cleave with *The Shard*, trailing a blazing crescent of teal planetary energy!  
*SFX:* `KRAAAAAAAA-SHRAAAAAAAK!`

**PANEL 2 (The Severing):**
The crescent blade cleanly slices through Governor Mujib’s titanium spinal rig, short-circuiting the crimson conduits in a violent shower of blue-gold sparks. Mujib’s black coat is torn across the chest; his magnum clatters to the floor.

**PANEL 3 (The Sovereign Remains Standing):**
The storm clears slightly. Rain pours through the open dome. Governor Mujib drops to one knee on the shattered carpet, breathing heavily, wiping a line of blood from his chin. His black horn-rimmed glasses are cracked, but his head remains held high, looking straight up into Kael’s eyes with an unbroken, piercing gaze.

**PANEL 4 (The Acknowledgment):**
Kael stands over him, chest heaving, *The Shard* resting inches from Mujib's throat. Kael lowers the blade.

**KAEL:**
*"Your war is over, Governor."*

**PANEL 5 (Mujib's Cold Smile):**
Governor Mujib reaches calmly into his pocket, pulls out his cyber-pipe, and strikes an amber spark. He exhales a slow trail of smoke into the rain and smiles.

**GOVERNOR MUJIB:**
*"You won the room, boy. You've got fire in your blood. Real fire."*  
*(He takes a slow pull from his pipe)*  
*"But the core beneath this city has already begun to shift. AXIOM's central armada is on its way. Let's see if your sword is big enough to hold back the sky."*

**PANEL 6 (The Exit to the Depths):**
Governor Mujib turns and walks calmly into his fortified private bunker elevator, the steel blast doors closing behind him. Kael turns toward the subterranean elevator shaft leading down into the living mantle.

**CAPTION:**
*The Sovereign of the surface had stepped aside.*  
*The true nightmare beneath the earth was waiting.*
