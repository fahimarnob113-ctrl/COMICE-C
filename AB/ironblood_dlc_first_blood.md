# IRONBLOOD: FIRST BLOOD — Origin Story & Character Bible

**Tagline:** *Before the block. Before the Siphon. Six strangers took a job that rewrote the future.*

---

## 🌍 SETTING & PREMISE

**Old Dhaka & The Sky-Scrap. 2067. Three years before the main game.**

AXIOM hasn't locked down Noakhali yet. They are consolidating their grip on the capital. Kael is still an enlisted district soldier doing off-book gigs. Jamal is a 16-year-old street runner. Ustad is a rogue freelance electrician. 

An anonymous broker hires six specialists independently for an impossible heist: **breach the sealed Old Dhaka Core** and extract a classified AXIOM manifest detailing their next occupation targets.

---

## 👤 CHARACTERS & PROMPTS

### 1. KPOP NINJA (The Sniper)
**Inspiration:** Gojo Satoru (Shinjuku Showdown vs Sukuna look) wearing Toji's iconic battle outfit + tactical sniper.
- **Look:** Tall, muscular, lean build. Wears a **tight black short-sleeved compression shirt** hugging his physique, paired with **loose, baggy off-white/cream martial arts pants** cinched with a black tactical belt and black slippers. 
- **Eyes & Face:** **No blindfold**. His eyes are wide open, blazing with an intense, boundless **electric sapphire blue glow**. Silver-white spiky hair. Confident, predatory grin.
- **Weapon:** A massive, custom matte-black **anti-materiel sniper rifle** carried one-handed or slung across his back.
- **Personality:** Insanely cocky, god-level reflexes, talks directly to an imaginary stream chat while casually hitting impossible shots. *"Chat, clip that."*
- **Copy/Paste Prompt:**
> `Character concept art, KPOP NINJA. A tall, muscular man with spiky silver-white hair and striking, wide-open glowing electric sapphire-blue eyes. Wearing a tight black short-sleeved compression shirt, loose baggy off-white martial arts trousers cinched with a black belt, and dark martial arts shoes. Cocky smirk, intense battle focus. Holding a massive matte-black anti-materiel sniper rifle with glowing blue barrel traces. Shinjuku Gojo vs Sukuna Toji outfit aesthetic, 16-bit arcade pixel art style, high contrast, sharp details.`

### 2. SUNEHARA (The Diva)
**Inspiration:** Mitsuri Kanroji warmth & energy + Bollywood action heroine.
- **Look:** Tall, athletic, long gradient hair (deep magenta roots fading to pastel pink tips). Custom ornate armored corset over a flowing, torn crimson and gold combat sari. Gold bangles (weighted throwing rings), armored thigh boots.
- **Weapon:** Segmented ribbon whip-sword.
- **Personality:** Aggressively kind, deadly graceful, compliments enemies while carving through them. Obsessed with high-protein sweet confections and fitness. Public face and cultural icon of the resistance.
- **Defining Lines:**
  - *"You're doing amazing, sweetie!"* (said to Kael while he's bleeding out)
  - *"I made saffron-pistachio protein sandesh! Forty grams of isolate per piece! Eat it before your muscles catabolize!"*
  - *[To an AXIOM soldier, genuinely concerned]* *"Are you sure you want to fight? Your form is terrible and your posture is screaming for magnesium."*
- **Copy/Paste Prompt:**
> `Character concept art, SUNEHARA THE DIVA. A tall, athletic, beautiful woman with long flowing hair in a gradient from magenta roots to pastel pink tips. Wearing a fitted armored corset over a torn, flowing crimson and gold combat sari. Gold bangles on wrists, thigh-high armored boots. Wielding a flexible segmented whip-sword. Cyberpunk anime warrior, Mitsuri Kanroji inspired, 16-bit arcade pixel art, vibrant.`

### 3. ZINNIA (The Toxicologist)
**Inspiration:** Shinobu Kocho's calm, smiling lethality + mature industrial biochemist.
- **Look:** Late 20s / early 30s, average/compact height (around 5'2"), poised Bengali woman with sharp elegant facial features. Long glossy dark hair styled in an intricate braided updo held by a slender silver needle pin (*kanta*). Wears a tailored, high-collared midnight-purple tactical tunic beneath a crisp, spotless double-breasted white lab coat. Thin gold round-wire glasses over dark, calm, unblinking eyes. Permanent, unsettlingly polite smile.
- **Weapon:** Pneumatic chemical injector pen & leather bandolier of bespoke glass shatter-vials (paralytics, corrosive acids, neurotoxins).
- **Personality:** Incurably polite, soft-spoken, terrifyingly clinical. Destroys heavily armored mechs with a drop of synthesized toxin. Never raises her voice; never loses her serene composure.
- **Copy/Paste Prompt:**
> `Character concept art, ZINNIA. A poised, average-height mature South Asian woman in her late 20s with sharp features, long dark hair in a braided updo with a silver needle hairpin, and thin round gold wireframe glasses. Wearing a tailored white lab coat over a high-collared dark purple tactical tunic with leather vial bandoliers. Calm, unsettlingly polite smile. Holding a sleek pneumatic medical injector pen with glowing violet liquid. Mature, compact, dignified, dangerous biochemist, 16-bit arcade pixel art style, high contrast, clean details.`

### 4. JAHANGIR BHAI (The Architect)
**Role:** The mastermind who hired all six strangers independently. He never shows his face during the mission — only revealing himself via secure comms at the end. He engineered the entire heist as an audition to build a resistance crew before AXIOM reaches Noakhali.

## 🗺️ LOCATIONS & AESTHETICS

1. **THE OLD DHAKA CORE (Acts I & II):** 
   Strict **monochrome** (black, white, thick grey ash-fog) with stark **neon red optics** on automated drones. Heavy, silent, eerie.
2. **THE SKY-SCRAP (Act III):** 
   Chaotic **warm orange diesel-punk** shantytown suspended in the clouds between skyscraper peaks. Zeppelins, smoke, howling wind, dynamic vertical platforming.
