## 🎨 Aesthetic: **Neon 80s on surface → progressively darker underground**

SURFACE  → Hot pink, electric cyan, acid yellow — pure neon 80s arcade
MID-RUIN → Orange rust neon, flicker and decay
UNDERGROUND → Teal bioluminescence, muted palette

## 👥 The Crew — Ally Roster

Eight allies, unlocked across the four acts. Not all playable — some are NPC support. Inspired by character references but fully original to the IRONBLOOD world.

---

### 1. 🍄 USTAD *(inspired by Mario)*
**Role**: Crew's heart. Technician. Comic relief that earns it.
- **Look**: Short, stocky, always wearing a worn work-helmet with a cracked visor. Coveralls. Tool belt.
- **Background**: City maintenance worker who kept The Ruin's pipes running. Knows every tunnel and duct in the system better than anyone. Never meant to be a fighter — keeps ending up in fights anyway.
- **Skill**: Opens locked routes, bypasses AXIOM systems, builds traps. In combat: wrench and shock-grenades.
- **Personality**: Warm, panicky under pressure, somehow always fine. The first to crack a joke, the last to give up.
- **Joins**: Act I — he's KAEL's contact who pulled the data from the AXIOM vault.

---

### 2. 🎮 JAMAL *(inspired by CJ — GTA: San Andreas)*
**Role**: Street intel. Mid-Ruin guide. The one who knows everybody.
- **Look**: Mid-height, athletic build. Layered street clothes, Iron Collar brand scar on his neck (escaped them). Backwards cap, worn sneakers.
- **Background**: Grew up in the Iron Collar's territory in Noakhali Block. Built his own small crew from scratch, resisted recruitment. When Iron Collar destroyed his block, he had nothing left to protect. Now he's the resistance's ear on the streets.
- **Skill**: Contacts everywhere. Can call in drops (ammo, health) mid-stage once per area. Fast close-quarters fighter — two short blades.
- **Personality**: Loyal to a fault once he trusts you. Street-cool exterior, big moral core. Calls KAEL "bhai" — the highest compliment he gives anyone.
- **Joins**: Act I end — KAEL needs to move through Iron Collar streets, JAMAL is the only way through.

---

### 3. 🤖 BORKOT *(inspired by Optimus Prime)*
**Role**: The moral center. Heavy muscle. Former AXIOM combat robot — reprogrammed, now self-aware.
- **Look**: Enormous humanoid mech — taller than KAEL by a head. Former AXIOM military-grade chassis, stripped of corporate insignia, battered and repainted matte black + dark red. One arm rebuilt from scavenged Iron Collar parts after battle damage. Glowing blue optics.
- **Background**: AXIOM's most advanced autonomous combat unit, deployed to suppress the Noakhali Block uprising. A resistance hacker named him BORKOT when they cracked his loyalty subroutines open. He chose to stay free. AXIOM wants him decommissioned — he's proof their AI can disobey.
- **Skill**: The tank. Heavy chassis absorbs enormous damage. Suppression fire, can physically carry two people out of danger. In cutscenes: the one who delivers the quiet, devastating moral argument.
- **Personality**: Speaks simply. Thinks deeply. Doesn't understand jokes but tries. The crew's most dependable member and the one KAEL trusts most — because BORKOT cannot lie.
- **Joins**: Act II — he finds KAEL, not the other way around.

---

### 4. 🟢 VOSS *(inspired by Piccolo)*
**Role**: The ex-enemy. Hollow Vein defector. Calculated and cold.
- **Look**: Lean, tall, grey-green tinted skin (a side effect of extended Hollow Vein rituals). Dark robes, but practical underneath. Bioluminescent markings on his forearms. No expression most of the time.
- **Background**: A senior Hollow Vein operative who realized the Vein isn't trying to *commune* with The Siphon — they're trying to *release* it. He came to KAEL not out of conscience but strategy: the Vein must be stopped, and KAEL is the only weapon capable.
- **Skill**: Knows every Hollow Vein tunnel, ritual, and creature weakness. Can suppress the Siphon-matter in KAEL's body (used narratively — he's why KAEL doesn't lose control permanently).
- **Personality**: Zero sentiment. Completely honest. The crew doesn't trust him. He doesn't care. Slowly, grudgingly, becomes the one KAEL actually listens to.
- **Joins**: Act II — ambushes KAEL, then offers the alliance.

---

### 💪 MASUD *(inspired by War Machine)*
**Role**: Heavy weapons. Military precision. The arsenal.
- **Look**: Former military, covered in salvaged and upgraded AXIOM tech armor. Not fancy — functional. Gatling-mounted shoulder rig bolted to the frame himself. Visor covers top half of face. Walks with a slight limp — old shrapnel, never got it removed.
- **Background**: Ex-Bangladeshi special forces, recruited by AXIOM's private army for high pay. Was hired to "clean up" a resistance cell in Chittagong Yards. He did the job. Then he found what AXIOM was actually doing with the bodies. He's been making it right, one building at a time, since.
- **Skill**: The firepower. Long-range support, suppression, demolition. In brawler stages, deploys cover fire that creates safe zones. Highest HP pool in the crew.
- **Personality**: Professional to a fault. Everything is a mission. Has a dry, deadpan humor that surfaces at the worst possible moments. Calls BORKOT "tin bhai" (tin brother).
- **Joins**: Act III — KAEL needs someone to blow a hole in AXIOM's underground excavation site wall.

---

### 🕴️ JAHANGIR BHAI *(inspired by Tommy Vercetti)*
**Role**: Surface crime lord. Unexpected ally. Knows where all the bodies are buried.
- **Look**: The only clean one in every room. Long tailored coat over body armor — looks like it came from Dubai Quarter's last luxury boutique. Silver-streaked hair, impeccable posture. Walks with a carved cane that conceals a blade. Looks like he belongs in AXIOM towers — that's entirely the point.
- **Background**: Controls what remains of organized trade on the surface — Noakhali Block to Dubai Quarter. Everyone calls him "Bhai" out of genuine respect or calculated fear, depending on who they are. Had a deal with AXIOM — until they tried to absorb his people. Nobody absorbs Jahangir Bhai.
- **Skill**: Resources and network. He can get anything — shipments, safehouses, information, people. Doesn't fight alongside KAEL (beneath his dignity) but opens every door money and reputation can buy.
- **Personality**: Warm, measured, always three steps ahead. Calls everyone "son" once. If he calls you "son" twice, you're in. The crew is wary of him. JAMAL grew up hearing his name.
- **Joins**: Act II — KAEL needs surface access that only Jahangir Bhai's network can provide.

---

### 🥁 MIRJA ABBAS *(inspired by Tengen Uzui)*
**Role**: The flashy one. Dual-weapon specialist. Stealth and spectacle.
- **Look**: Tall, impossibly theatrical. Wears layered salvaged armor that somehow looks intentional — adorned with scavenged rings, chains, and one gold tooth. Dual oversized modified rebar cleavers. *"If I die today, I die looking magnificent."*
- **Background**: Ex-resistance performer — ran broadcast raids, hijacking AXIOM's own feeds to show the city what fighting back looked like. AXIOM burned his stage and arrested his crew. He went dark for two years. Now he's back, the raids are bigger, and he remembered everyone's face.
- **Skill**: Speed and spectacle. Theatrical combo attacks that create openings. Can distract entire enemy squads by the sheer audacity of his presence. Locally famous — some enemies pause before attacking him.
- **Personality**: The biggest ego in the room, and earns every bit of it. Everything is a performance. Somehow the most sincere person once the show drops. Calls BORKOT "brother" and means it.
- **Joins**: Act I end — his broadcast is what first brings KAEL's resistance cell any public attention.

---

### 🪂 RUSSIAN JAHANGIR *(inspired by Rico Rodriguez — Just Cause)*
**Role**: The chaos agent. Outside operative. Shows up when things need to explode.
- **Look**: Light tactical gear — built for speed. Grapple-hook rig on left arm, repurposed from an AXIOM elevator system. Always carrying more explosives than seems safe or legal. Fur-lined collar on his jacket — old habit from somewhere very cold.
- **Background**: Nobody is entirely sure. Came in from outside — somewhere Eastern European by the accent, but he's been everywhere. Had a contract, saw what AXIOM was building, decided the demolition job was bigger than the original client. The original client is probably also demolished by now.
- **Skill**: Mobility and demolition. Goes vertical when everyone else goes horizontal. Grapple-throw combat — grabs enemies, throws them at each other or off ledges. Opens impassable routes through sheer explosive persistence.
- **Personality**: Calls everything "beautiful" right before it explodes. Speaks Bengali with a bizarre Russian accent that somehow works. Refers to JAHANGIR BHAI as "my brother, different mother." Nobody understands their relationship.
- **Joins**: Act III — drops in from above during a mission. Nobody invited him. JAHANGIR BHAI just shrugs.

## 📡 THE DIRECTIVE INSIDER — Whistleblower NPC

**Codename**: CIPHER
**Real name**: Dr. Rana Hossain *(never spoken in-game — found only in one encrypted file late in Act III)*

**Role**: AXIOM's own people, feeding the resistance from the inside.

- **Who she is**: A mid-level data architect inside Project IRONBLOOD. She designed the containment protocols for the Siphon extraction system — and when she understood what it actually was and what it would do to the city's remaining population, she started documenting everything.
- **Look**: Never seen in person. Exists as a *distorted voice on intercepted AXIOM comms*, a blinking cursor in broken terminals, a single gloved hand pushing a data chip under a door in one cutscene. The player never sees her face. Her voice is calm, technical, precise — like she's filing a report she knows will get her killed.
- **Delivery mechanic**: CIPHER's intel arrives as:
  - Intercepted audio fragments on broken AXIOM terminals (KAEL stops, listens)
  - Encrypted data chips hidden in unlikely places — taped under a dead guard's collar, inside a food ration can
  - One live transmission mid-Act II where she talks directly to KAEL for 90 seconds, then the signal cuts
- **What she provides**:
  - AXIOM facility blueprints (unlocks alternate routes in stages)
  - Project IRONBLOOD documentation (lore drops, the truth about what KAEL is)
  - The Siphon's physical location — information no one else has
- **Her arc**: She goes silent in Act III. KAEL finds a terminal with one last encrypted file — coordinates, and a single line: *"Don't let it wake up. — R"* Whether she's dead, in hiding, or extracted by JAHANGIR BHAI's network is never confirmed.
- **Why she matters narratively**: She is the only character in the game who chose AXIOM, understood it fully, and still chose to burn it. Everyone else had no choice. She did.

## 👹 THE SIPHON — Final Boss

**Reference**: Steppenwolf (DC — Justice League)

### Design
- **Scale**: Twice KAEL's height. Armored entirely in layered organic-metal plating — like bone fused with black iron, covered in bioluminescent Hollow Vein markings (corrupted teal-green)
- **Face**: Partially visible — ancient, sunken, inhuman. Eyes are empty white voids. No mouth — communicates in a vibration you feel more than hear.
- **Weapon**: A massive war-axe made of compressed Siphon-matter — hits the ground, sends shockwave fissures of dark energy outward. (Steppenwolf's axe energy, but black and earth-cracking)
- **Presence**: When it appears on screen, the neon colors of the HUD desaturate. The CRT effect intensifies. The screen itself seems to contract.

### Boss Fight — Three Phases
| Phase | State | Attack Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | Dormant form — still partially tethered to the earth | Slow, devastating axe swings. Ground fissures. Summons Hollow Vein cultists. |
| **Phase 2** | Awakening — stands fully, armor expands | Faster axe combos. Energy beam from chest. Grabs and crushes. |
| **Phase 3** | Full Siphon — armor cracks, blinding teal light pours out | Frantic, pattern-based, screen goes near-black except for attack tells. KAEL's rage form is the only thing that dents it. |

### The Twist
> The Siphon recognizes KAEL. It *remembers* its shard. During Phase 3, it stops attacking briefly — and offers to take it back. The player keeps fighting. That's the answer.

## 🎨 Asset Style — CONFIRMED

> **Classic GBA-era pixel art. No heavy textures. Keep it clean and readable.**

- All sprites: pixel art, small resolution (16×16 to 48×48 px per character frame)
- No gradients on sprites — flat color blocking with 1-pixel shading
- Animations: 4–8 frames per action (walk, attack, hurt, idle)
- Tiles: 16×16 px grid, clean edges
- VFX: pixel particle bursts, no soft glows on sprites (glow only on HUD/UI via Canvas shadowBlur)
- **Asset pipeline**: AI-generated concept → pixel-converted → imported as sprite sheet
